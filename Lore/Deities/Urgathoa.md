Urgathoa, the Pallid Princess, is the goddess of disease, gluttony, and undeath. She calls her followers to pursue every sensation and desire without regard for morality, social restraint, or mortality.

## Origins & Nature

### The First Undead

Urgathoa was once a mortal woman devoted to the pleasures of body and mind. Rather than accept death, she fled [[Pharasma]]'s River of Souls and tore herself free from the [[Boneyard]], becoming the first undead. The psychopomps sent against her failed, and their defeat culminated in her ascension as an exemplar of appetite unbound by mortal limits.

### Manifestation

Urgathoa appears as a pale, raven-haired woman wielding a scythe. The flesh of her torso peels away into exposed bone, while her legs are formed from bleached white bones. Her divine symbol is a death's-head moth.

### Bloodrot

Urgathoa maintains a necropolis in [[Bloodrot]], a territory she controls within [[Abaddon]]. Her domain lies alongside those of the [[Four Horsemen]].

## Faith & Practices

### Indulgence and Affliction

Urgathoans place personal gratification before all other concerns. Some seek rare delicacies, rot, blood, or flesh; others mix pleasure with pain while pursuing increasingly extreme experiences. Many willingly contract illnesses, treating infection and fever as novel sensations and possible sources of visions from their goddess. Favored messengers may remain perpetually sick.

Followers are also expected to spread disease and rot. The faith interprets germs multiplying within a host and organisms consuming decaying matter as forms of gluttony practiced throughout nature.

### Undeath and Violence

The faith presents undeath as the ultimate escape from mortality. Its followers pursue transformation, study necromancy, venerate existing undead, or offer themselves as food to ravenous devotees. Its most violent adherents torture and massacre followers of rival gods, then reanimate victims who opposed undeath and turn them against surviving loved ones.

### Hidden Worship

Urgathoans usually conceal their faith, and dedicated temples are rare. Those that exist are often feasting halls established near graveyards and staffed by undead. The faith's holy text, [[Serving Your Hunger]], is a cookbook of forbidden recipes and riddles intended to foster acceptance of undeath.

During the reaping ritual, a priest goes out to kill as many victims as possible, then presents blood-soaked robes to Urgathoa as an offering.

## Worshippers & Influence

### Centers of Worship

Urgathoa's worship is strongest in [[Ustalav]] and [[Geb]].

### Prominent Figures

- [[Barasthangas]] creates undead infused with the entropic power of the [[Void]].
- [[Fjarn]] is a fallen Linnorm King who wages war as a ghoul commander.
- [[Mother's Maw]] serves as Urgathoa's divine herald and direct emissary.
- [[Many-Limbs Kimmato]] is a former Sarenite physician transformed through grief and blasphemy.
- [[Urra Lynedon]] cultivates diseases for other members of the faith.

### Divine Servitors

[[Sacrovalt|Sacrovalts]] serve Urgathoa by feeding on souls within her realm.

### Cults

- The [[Savoring Society]] turns Absalom's wealth and privilege into prolonged excess.
- The [[Ashen Church]] offers undeath as compassionate release from fear and suffering.
- The [[Shepherds of Affliction]] use disease to decide who deserves to survive.
- The [[Eternal Eaters]] consume people, valuables, and other materials for an imagined afterlife.

## Connections

- [[Illcayna Alonnor]] seeks to turn [[Isger]] toward Urgathoa by force.
- [[Luminia Bell]] secretly directs Urgathoan worship in [[Elidir]].
- [[Sarenrae]] and her faithful are particular targets of Many-Limbs Kimmato.
- [[Pharasma]] despises Urgathoa as the first undead and a defier of the River of Souls.
- [[Zyphus]], god of accidental death, is Urgathoa's closest divine ally and resides in Bloodrot.
- Urgathoa has few other divine allies beyond fellow undead deities and the [[Four Horsemen]].
