The Battle of Hellknight Hill was a major clash near [[Breachill]] and [[Citadel Altaerein]] between Chelaxian [[Hellknights]] and Andoren-backed forces. Some see it as the first salvo in a possible war between [[Cheliax]] and [[Andoran]].

## Background

The battle grew out of the [[Order of the Rack]] occupation of Breachill, launched after Isgeri officials suspected the town or nearby [[Citadel Altaerein]] held hidden warshards belonging to the [[Hellbreakers League]]. At [[Hedvend VI]]'s request, Abrogail Thrune II sent loyalist Rack Hellknights in Abadius 4725 AR to root out treason and reclaim any warshards they found. By Gozran 4725 AR, the Hellknights occupied Breachill, placed most citizens under house arrest, and carried out public executions without finding League members.

## Course

In Sarenith 4725 AR, Andoran sent diplomats led by semiretired General Reginald Cormoth to request that the Hellknights stop subjugating Breachill. The Hellknights captured and publicly executed him, claiming without evidence that he was an enemy spy, while his retinue fled. Public outrage led to an emergency People's Council in [[Almas]] in Erastus 4725 AR, after which Eagle Knights of the [[Steel Falcons]] traveled to Isger.

In Arodus 4725 AR, Chelaxian supplies allowed the Order of the Rack to fortify Citadel Altaerein with infernal wards and a conjured diabolical dragon. In Rova 4725 AR, Andoren forces fought through grueling exchanges to retake Breachill and the citadel.

## Participants

- [[Order of the Rack]]: Chelaxian Hellknights occupying Breachill and Citadel Altaerein.
- [[Andoran]]: Sent diplomats and later Eagle Knights after Reginald Cormoth's execution.
- [[Breachill]]: Town whose council resisted the occupation.
- [[Hellbreakers League]]: Rebel organization whose rumored presence triggered the crackdown.

## Outcome & Legacy

Andoran's forces won, retaking Breachill and Citadel Altaerein. Some Rack Hellknights were taken prisoner, while many others retreated toward [[Cheliax]]. The [[Hellbreakers League]] emerged from nearby mountain caves to negotiate with Andoran, which promised aid in exchange for a powerful warshard taken back to Andoran. The defeat caused troop movements across Isger, including soldiers returning to [[Elidir]], Hellknights gathering in [[Logas]], and [[Wolfpoint]] being left empty.
