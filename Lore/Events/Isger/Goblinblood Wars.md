The Goblinblood Wars were a devastating four-year conflict in [[Isger]] from 4697 to 4701 AR. Hobgoblin warlords mobilized hundreds of goblinoids from the [[Chitterwood]], and the war killed thousands of Isgeri citizens while causing immense property damage.

## Background

The conflict began after Chelaxians encroached on the Chitterwood. What started as self-defense grew into all-out war as hobgoblin leaders seized the opportunity, drawing in goblins angered by human mistreatment and others conscripted by force.

## Course

Goblin hordes launched attacks across the nation and moved north across the [[Conerica River]] toward the foothills of the [[Five Kings Mountains]]. The Isgeri government was slow to react to the brutal conflict in the countryside, deepening resentment among rural civilians. The offensive was stopped only by an unlikely alliance of Cheliax's [[Hellknights]], Druma's Mercenary League, and Andoran's Eagle Knights, who burned the edges of the Chitterwood and reduced the forest's size.

## Participants

- [[Chitterwood]] goblin tribes and hobgoblin warlords formed the invading armies.
- [[Hellknights]], Druma's Mercenary League, and Andoran's Eagle Knights halted the offensive.
- [[Isgeri Army]] and rural Isgeri communities bore the consequences of the war.

## Outcome & Legacy

The war left Isger's army devastated, forcing [[Hedvend VI]] to concentrate soldiers on vital trade routes while rural communities defended themselves. It also left wounded veterans, orphans, destroyed towns, and lasting distrust of goblinoids. The [[Church of Asmodeus]], [[House Thrune]], and the [[Sisterhood of the Golden Erinys]] gained influence through postwar aid, while [[Illcayna Alonnor]] exploited the casualties to raise more undead. The trauma also fed groups such as the [[Isgeri Slayers]] and left communities such as [[Logas]], [[Gillamoor]], [[Good Home]], and [[Vordia]] permanently changed.
