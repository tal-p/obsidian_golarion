The Godsrain sparked revolutionary hope in [[Isger]] by proving that even the mightiest gods could fall. For many Isgeri, that realization made [[Cheliax]] and [[House Thrune]] seem less inevitable.

## Background

Isger was already strained by centuries of foreign rule, rural neglect, and the unresolved damage of the [[Goblinblood Wars]]. The Godsrain gave dissidents a symbol and a sense that history could change.

## Course

When [[Gorum]] died in Neth 4724 AR, the Godsrain scattered [[Warshard|warshards]] across Golarion and began a scramble to claim them. The [[Hellbreakers League]] discovered one and used it as a rallying symbol. [[Illcayna Alonnor]] and [[Lady Kaltessa Iyis]] also collected warshards for their own purposes, while a fragment found at [[Twin Horns]] affected the nunnery's goats, turning them into dawnsilver.

## Participants

- [[Hellbreakers League]]: Turned a warshard into a revolutionary banner.
- [[Cheliax]]: Sought control over warshards and moved against Isgeri rebels.
- [[Illcayna Alonnor]]: Collected warshards for her undead designs.
- [[Lady Kaltessa Iyis]]: Acquired a Warshard for her own schemes.
- [[Twin Horns]]: Site of a Warshard fragment's strange effect.

## Outcome & Legacy

The Godsrain escalated Isger's unrest into a warshard arms race. Isgeri officials suspected that the Hellbreakers were hiding warshards in [[Breachill]] or [[Citadel Altaerein]], and Abrogail Thrune II sent [[Order of the Rack]] Hellknights to suppress the Hellbreakers and reclaim any fragments they found. That crackdown led toward the [[Battle of Hellknight Hill]]. The Godsrain also emboldened opportunists such as Kaltessa and unsettled communities such as [[Merrywim]].
