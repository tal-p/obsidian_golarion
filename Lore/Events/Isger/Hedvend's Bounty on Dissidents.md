Hedvend's Bounty on Dissidents was a nationwide reward offered by [[Hedvend VI]] for the identification or capture of "dissenters and revolutionaries." Issued after the [[Battle of Hellknight Hill]], its deliberately vague terms encouraged private citizens and Chelaxian loyalists to hunt suspected opponents of [[Cheliax]] on the steward's behalf.

## Background

The defeat of the [[Order of the Rack]] at [[Breachill]] and [[Citadel Altaerein]] gave the [[Hellbreakers League]] unprecedented momentum. Hedvend responded from [[Elidir]] by attempting to suppress anti-Chelaxian activity throughout [[Isger]], while League founders [[Alceo Demetrias]] and [[Phoebe Demetrias]] quietly sought support within the government to have the decree rescinded.

## Course

The decree offered a sizable but unspecified reward for information about dissidents and revolutionaries without defining either category. Copies were posted outside Breachill's town hall and soon appeared on roadside inns, trees, fence posts, farms, and villages across the countryside. Its wording effectively invited citizens to accuse neighbors, settle old grudges, and collect payment under color of law.

The bounty transformed ordinary political suspicion into organized vigilantism. Chelaxian loyalists in [[Draymoor]] questioned strangers traveling from Breachill. Loyalists arriving by ship from [[Logas]] infiltrated [[Everbloom Imports Warehouse]], captured [[Gertraud Mildenhall]], and waited for more Hellbreakers in hopes of increasing their reward. Elsewhere, informants offered genuine or invented intelligence to the authorities.

The Chelaxian naval captain [[Lamperia Bane]] escalated the policy by promising to double Hedvend's reward for useful information. This induced one of [[Rhokrag]]'s contacts to betray him at the Waterspout Inn, where Bane captured and tortured the hobgoblin in an effort to expose the League's members, activities, and headquarters.

## Participants

- [[Hedvend VI]] - Issued the bounty as a decentralized instrument for suppressing opposition to Chelaxian rule.
- [[Hellbreakers League]] - Primary target of the decree and of loyalists seeking its reward.
- [[Alceo Demetrias]] and [[Phoebe Demetrias]] - Worked within Elidir to pressure Hedvend into rescinding the policy.
- [[Lamperia Bane]] - Chelaxian naval captain who doubled the offered reward and used it to recruit informants.
- **Chelaxian loyalists and private informants** - Questioned travelers, raided suspected rebel properties, betrayed contacts, and pursued personal profit under the decree.

## Outcome & Legacy

The bounty spread fear beyond Breachill and made political affiliation dangerous throughout Isger. Even communities sympathetic to the Battle of Hellknight Hill worried that neighboring settlements might identify them as rebels. It also blurred government enforcement with private vengeance: accusations required little proof, loyalists operated without meaningful oversight, and the prospect of payment rewarded escalation.

The policy failed to halt the Hellbreakers. Its abuses instead demonstrated the steward's willingness to turn Isgeri against one another, reinforcing the League's argument that Hedvend governed for Cheliax rather than for Isger.
