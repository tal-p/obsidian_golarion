The Shepherds of Affliction are an [[Eye of Dread]] cult that uses disease to decide who deserves to survive civilization's next catastrophe. They revere [[Urgathoa]] as the pinnacle of survival and seek to build a world populated by those who endure their trials.

## Purpose & Beliefs

The Shepherds believe ordinary mortals are helpless before godlike threats and that civilization can survive only by nurturing the strong and removing the weak. Disease is their chosen test: survival demonstrates worth, while death justifies a victim's reduction to an undead tool.

## Structure & Influence

The cult travels between settlements, infecting entire communities and withholding medicine. Survivors are recruited, fed, trained for war, and taught necromantic secrets; the dead are reanimated as servants rather than given peaceful rest.

Those who receive visions of Urgathoa during illness become privileged prophets, though the Shepherds keep them perpetually fevered. Each tested settlement supplies either new members or new undead, allowing the movement to grow as it marches toward the diseased world it considers necessary.

## Connections

- The [[Eye of Dread]] shaped the cult's belief that only ruthless preparation can answer overwhelming threats.
- [[Urgathoa]] embodies the survival, disease, and undeath around which its society is organized.
