The Eternal Eaters are a [[Golden Road]] cult whose members believe everything they consume becomes part of their souls and follows them into the afterlife. Their meals are efforts to furnish an eternal existence rather than merely sustain mortal bodies.

## Purpose & Beliefs

Members swallow heirlooms and gems to fill otherworldly coffers, and consume stone and metal to build imagined palaces. The most extreme eat beloved animals or family members so they can never be separated in death. Every act is dedicated to [[Urgathoa]] in the hope that she will receive them into her domain.

## Structure & Influence

The cult draws followers from across the Golden Road rather than from a single community. Their impossible diets cause severe suffering, but members reject treatment because they consider the pain a minor cost of everlasting happiness. Upon death, each Eternal Eater gives their body to Urgathoa so it can continue serving her while the soul enters its carefully provisioned afterlife.

## Connections

- [[Urgathoa]] is asked to preserve both the worshipper and everything they consumed.
- The [[Golden Road]] supplies the cult's geographically scattered membership.
