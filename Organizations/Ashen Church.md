The Ashen Church is a widespread cult of [[Urgathoa]] that presents undeath as a compassionate escape from mortality. Its gentle missionaries appear amid war, disaster, and poverty, offering comfort where fear and desperation have taken hold.

## Purpose & Beliefs

The Church argues that mortality drives people toward fear and cruelty, while Urgathoa offers release from that cycle. It teaches that death need not be an ending and describes undeath as a mercy available to anyone willing to accept it.

Its compassion extends to undead creatures, which members treat with respect and affection rather than revulsion. This calm acceptance can ease the aggression of otherwise ravenous undead and reinforces the cult's claim that coexistence is possible.

## Structure & Influence

The Ashen Church establishes itself wherever suffering has made communities receptive to its message. Members rely on kindness and empathy rather than terror, opening people to Urgathoa's faith before revealing the full implications of the immortality they promise. This approach has made the Church one of the most successful cults devoted to spreading undeath.

## Connections

- [[Urgathoa]] is presented as a merciful answer to mortal fear.
- Wars, disasters, and impoverished communities provide the Church with its most receptive audiences.
