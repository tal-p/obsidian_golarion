The Isgeri Army is a volunteer military force that [[Cheliax]] requires [[Isger]] to maintain. It protects important trade routes, borders, and river traffic, but Cheliax provides little money, materiel, or training.

## Purpose & Beliefs

The army watches over travel on the [[Conerica River]] and patrols the borders with [[Druma]] and [[Andoran]]. Its soldiers are caught between local needs and the demands of a vassal government that often neglects the countryside.

## Structure & Influence

General Harwin Balefellow leads the army. He is principled and frustrated by Isger's condition, but he follows orders closely and secretly hopes for change without wanting to be the person who forces it.

## Connections

- [[Hedvend VI]] oversees Isger's government from [[Elidir]], where many soldiers have been pulled back since the [[Battle of Hellknight Hill]].
- [[Gillamoor]] was briefly quarantined by the army after its undead disaster was mistaken for plague.
- [[Wolfpoint]] is a reserve garrison currently empty because its soldiers were called elsewhere.
