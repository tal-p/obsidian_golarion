The Hookback tribe is a deep-cavern goblin tribe of the southern [[Chitterwood]]. Its people are known for ceremonial piercings, expert underground foraging, and a culture that prefers darkness to the surface world.

## History

The tribe's defining piercings began in wartime trauma. During the [[Goblinblood Wars]], Hookback survivors endured brutal torture and were left ashamed of the piercings forced on them. A young goblin later chose to be pierced in honor of the people's pain, turning the marks into a rite of passage.

Hookbacks see their piercings as a sign of survival and adulthood. They regard creatures of darkness as kin and fear the bright surface, where light is painful and exposure feels dangerous. Fire is not a plaything to them; it is feared and revered as the greatest light, the force that destroyed their old homes, and the tool used for piercings and important ceremonies.

## Structure & Influence

The tribe lives in the [[Sunless Tunnels]], where its members have learned to see in deep darkness and listen for the faintest footsteps. They forage, farm mushrooms and lichen, and gather fish and newts. Their tunnels are rich in ores used for piercings, and the tribe has mastered specialized smelting despite its limited use of fire.

## Connections

- [[Sunless Tunnels]] are the tribe's home and source of metal for piercings.
- [[Goblinblood Wars]] transformed forced torture into a central cultural rite.
