The People of the Swarm are one of Isger's largest and most prominent goblin tribes, known for group tactics, resistance songs, hatred of hobgoblin commanders, and their bat-haunted home in the [[Shrieking Caverns]].

## History

The tribe learned early that goblins working together were more likely to survive fights and seize treasure. Swarm goblins prize numbers, coordination, song, and survival through collective action.

During and after the [[Goblinblood Wars]], the tribe lost many warriors and fled into the caverns. Elders sang of the war, lost heroes, old lives, bear attacks, and resilience, preserving the belief that goblins will become many again while hobgoblins become few. Their songs include nearly two hundred warnings about bears and cave bears, born from hard lessons about the limits of swarming a larger predator.

## Structure & Influence

The tribe found its home after disturbing a vast bat colony whose noise and chaos felt like a sign of safety. Swarm goblins compare themselves to bats: sleeping only where safe, foraging at night, and leaving their caves in groups.

They live high in the Shrieking Caverns, sleeping in goblin-sized nooks and crannies above floodwaters and away from cave bears. Children are kept in protected enclosures near adult sleeping places. Swarm goblins learn to climb almost as soon as they can walk.

The tribe adapted its tactics to cave life, using height, sound, and group movement. Inspired by bats, members developed clicks, chirps, and other sounds as a private method of communication in the dark.

## Connections

- [[Shrieking Caverns]] are the tribe's home and source of its bat-inspired habits.
- [[Goblinblood Wars]] and hobgoblin command remain central themes in the tribe's songs.
