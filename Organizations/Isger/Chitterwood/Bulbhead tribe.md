The Bulbhead tribe is a [[Chitterwood]] goblin tribe of the [[Burnt Ends]], known for gourd masks, quick-moving camps, cautious trade with outsiders, and a fierce feud with the [[Isgeri Slayers]].

## History

During the [[Goblinblood Wars]], hobgoblin commanders forced some goblin troops to wear gourds filled with wasps or spiders. The Bulbheads reclaimed the practice by wearing gourd masks and helmets voluntarily, turning old humiliation into a symbol of identity and pride. Even babies wear tiny gourds, and the tribe is famous for pumpkin dishes, mashed gourd and rat stew, and caramelized pumpkin squares.

## Structure & Influence

The tribe is loosely nomadic, moving along the Chitterwood's borders in tents covered with leaves and sticks for camouflage. Camps may last a week or so before moving, and the tribe marks useful sites with patches of gourds so they can return later for harvest.

Because they live near the forest edge, Bulbheads interact with non-goblinoids more often than many Chitterwood tribes. They set up temporary stalls to trade forest goods with merchants and abandon them immediately when danger appears. They can be warm hosts to open-handed visitors, but they are ruthless toward enemies and treat conflict with the Isgeri Slayers as an ongoing war.

## Connections

- [[Burnt Ends]] is the tribe's home territory along the recovering Chitterwood edge.
- [[Isgeri Slayers]] are a hated enemy; Bulbheads raid their camps, leave gourds as taunts, and sometimes burn Slayer tents.
