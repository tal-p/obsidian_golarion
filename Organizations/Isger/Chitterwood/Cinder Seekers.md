The Cinder Seekers are a small but fervent goblin movement in the [[Chitterwood]] devoted to remembering the [[Goblinblood Wars]] and preventing goblins from ever serving hobgoblins again.

## Purpose & Beliefs

Cinder Seekers preserve history through dirges about hobgoblin oppression and goblin suffering. Their songs can flatten the war into a simple story of hobgoblin villains and goblin victims, ignoring that some goblin tribes willingly fought their humanoid neighbors.

They treat fire as a purifying force that can burn away both enemies and the will to harm goblins. Many view all hobgoblins with suspicion; the most zealous attack indiscriminately, while wiser members try to judge intent first.

## Structure & Influence

The movement grew from older goblins' memories of hobgoblin cruelty and remains strongest among those generations. Its members travel through the Chitterwood warning other goblins against hobgoblin domination, punishing those they see as repeating the past, and using arson against hobgoblins and goblin collaborators. They tend the [[Big Fire]], which they regard as nearly sacred even though its endurance depends on constant care.

## Connections

- [[Big Fire]] is the movement's central sacred landmark and source of ritual flame.
- [[Goblinblood Wars]] define the movement's memory, songs, and vengeance.
- [[Hobgoblins]] are the focus of the movement's suspicion and violence.
