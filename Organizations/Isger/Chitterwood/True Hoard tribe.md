The True Hoard tribe is a Chitterwood goblin tribe of scavengers, trap makers, inventors, and alchemists. Its members rarely throw anything away, especially if it might one day prove useful or is simply shiny enough to count as treasure.

## History

The [[Goblinblood Wars]] nearly destroyed the tribe's practical knowledge when skilled hunters and trap makers were conscripted by hobgoblin commanders and died in battle. Survivors passed on what they could, and later generations rediscovered much through trial, error, and cavern life.

True Hoard goblins value curiosity, improvisation, salvage, and reuse. In the caverns, turning junk into tools and scraps into food can mean survival. Their treasured objects need not be useful; beauty, shine, or strangeness can be enough.

## Structure & Influence

The tribe is more focused on gathering, scavenging, stealing, and making than on fighting, though its members can hunt and defend themselves well. They are especially talented at snares, nets, clever traps, alchemy, and invention. Many goblin alchemists and inventors encountered in Isger have ties to the True Hoard.

Their formulas and notes are not recorded in ordinary writing, making them difficult for outsiders to interpret.

The tribe has an odd relationship with bushy-tailed woodrats, which they call gully-rakers. Both goblins and woodrats collect shiny and interesting objects, leading to regular clashes over flood-borne debris and hidden nests. Experiments with woodrat waste have even led to useful discoveries.

## Connections

- [[Chitterwood]] and its caverns supply the tribe's debris, scraps, flood treasures, and woodrats.
- [[Goblinblood Wars]] nearly erased the tribe's old expertise.
