The Mudrub tribe is a small goblin tribe of the swampy eastern [[Chitterwood]]. Its members coat themselves in muck, hide in floodlands, and move through mud and brush with frog-like ease.

## History

During the [[Goblinblood Wars]], hobgoblins punished Mudrub goblins by branding them with words. Survivors burned and scarred over the brands to remove the shame and the hated writing, then transformed the practice into a tradition of artful scars and images rather than letters.

Mudrub goblins reject writing and cover their scarred bodies in a thick layer of swamp muck. Removing the muck to reveal their burn patterns is a rare sign of intimacy and respect. They jokingly call their children tadpoles and describe coming of age as becoming a frog.

## Structure & Influence

The tribe prefers hiding to fighting. Mudrub goblins use mud to mask their scent, lie still in banks for hours, and communicate from afar with bird calls. They live in tall mud huts built to endure flooding season, with rock paths that serve as stepping stones when waters rise.

Their diet relies heavily on frogs caught by hand from camouflage, though they also eat snakes and birds. Pickled frog legs and fried snake heads are common favorites.

## Connections

- [[Frog Bog]] is the tribe's home in the flood-prone eastern Chitterwood.
- [[Goblinblood Wars]] created the branding trauma that became Mudrub scar tradition.
