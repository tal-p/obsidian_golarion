The Spine Threshers are a Chitterwood goblin tribe famed for mounted combat, territorial ferocity, and devotion to [[Zarongel]]. They live in the [[Barking Caverns]], where they train riders and mounts through wide chambers and cramped racing tunnels.

## History

During the [[Goblinblood Wars]], hobgoblin commanders used Spine Threshers for especially vicious mounted attacks and cared little whether riders or mounts survived. Few warriors lived through the war, but the survivors were determined that the tribe's knowledge would not vanish.

The tribe traces its founding to a warrior whose mount was a gift from Zarongel in reward for skill and devotion. Spine Threshers treat mounted skill as devotion to Zarongel and prize hunting, dares, combat, and personal reputation. Cave bears inspire awe as models of battle ferocity, though the tribe wisely does not try to train them.

## Structure & Influence

The tribe claims one of the larger cavern territories, much of it dedicated to mounts, warriors, and training. Preserving the tribe after the war required rebuilding both the goblin population and the animals central to its culture.

Young riders prove themselves by racing through narrow natural tunnels, a dangerous pastime that sharpens skill and channels restlessness. Spine Threshers are often the goblins most likely to sneak aboveground for a risky dare. They are aggressively territorial and rarely let a fight end until they are certain the enemy is dead.

## Connections

- [[Barking Caverns]] are the tribe's home and training ground.
- [[Goblinblood Wars]] nearly destroyed the tribe's warriors and mounted traditions.
- [[Zarongel]] is the tribe's favored creator god.
