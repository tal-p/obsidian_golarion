The Stonechewer tribe is a goblin and hobgoblin community in [[Saringallow]]. Its members were among the city's first recent goblinoid immigrants, part of a deliberate effort to avert renewed conflict by welcoming the peoples of the [[Chitterwood]].

## History

The Stonechewers struggled to find anyone willing to rent them housing. Rather than leave, they decided to build their own homes and established the neighborhood now called [[Chitterstone Road]].

## Purpose & Beliefs

The tribe's community is built around self-sufficiency, skilled work, and mutual support in the face of discrimination. Its success has helped turn a resettlement experiment into a durable part of Saringallow.

## Structure & Influence

Mashnom, a mute female goblin union leader, directs the Stonechewers. Their businesses provide everyday goods, construction services, excellent masonry, and mercenary labor, making the community economically valuable beyond its own members.

## Connections

- [[Chitterstone Road]] is the neighborhood the tribe built.
- [[Saringallow]] paid some early goblinoid settlers to move into the city as part of its peace strategy.
- [[Chitterwood]] is the wider homeland whose peoples Saringallow hopes to reconcile with.
