The Sisterhood of the Golden Erinys is an Asmodean charitable order active across rural [[Isger]]. It became influential after the [[Goblinblood Wars]] by running orphanages and aid programs backed by [[House Thrune]] and the [[Church of Asmodeus]].

## History

After the war, orphaned children were placed in newly built sisterhood orphanages. The order spent the following decades raising those children within Asmodean doctrine, turning relief work into long-term religious influence.

## Purpose & Beliefs

The sisterhood presents its work as charity, but its programs bind recipients to Asmodean faith and Chelaxian loyalty. Many rural Isgeri are grateful for the help, while others view the arrangement as exploitative.

## Structure & Influence

[[Sister Marcella]] is the most influential member, serving as grand overseer of the sisterhood's charities. The order also maintains [[Twin Horns]], where many girls raised in its orphanages train as nuns.

## Connections

- [[Vordia]] is home to one of the first orphanages built by the sisterhood after the war.
- [[Twin Horns]] trains aspiring sisters in religious discipline and martial arts.
- [[Church of Asmodeus]] and [[Cheliax]] support and benefit from the sisterhood's work.
