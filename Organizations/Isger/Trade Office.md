The Trade Office is one of the two main forces empowered by [[Hedvend VI]] to keep [[Elidir]] functioning under Chelaxian rule. It oversees the capital's trade apparatus and works closely with the [[Isgeri Army]] to keep commerce flowing through the [[Conerica River]].

## Purpose & Beliefs

The office's purpose is practical rather than ideological: protect revenue, regulate movement through the capital, and ensure the labor supply remains reliable. It manages docks, warehouses, taxes, tariffs, fees, caravanserai, and other trade matters.

## Structure & Influence

The Trade Office operates from the Trade House, a sprawling labyrinth of buildings near [[Conerica Landing]]. Its wide authority leaves room for corruption, which is often ignored unless it becomes too visible. Even exposed corruption is punished lightly enough that many officials act with confidence.

## Connections

- [[Hedvend VI]] relies on the office to keep Cheliax satisfied with Isger's trade.
- [[Isgeri Army]] protects the Conerica Straits while the office manages commerce.
- [[Adasely Tisandera]] seeks power in the caravanserai market the office oversees.
- [[Queen of Conerica]] undermines the office's work by raiding trade ships.
