The Isgeri Slayers are an anti-goblin group founded by veterans and bigots after the [[Goblinblood Wars]]. They present themselves as mercenaries and adventurers, but their real purpose is vengeance against goblinkind.

## History

Varskand Gilwrad, one of the group's founders, lost his home and left eye to hobgoblin raiders during the war and never forgave goblinkind.

## Purpose & Beliefs

The Slayers see themselves as crusaders doing the work the government will not do. Their idea of justice is rooted in collective blame against goblins and goblinoids.

## Structure & Influence

Varskand works to make the Slayers appear legitimate rather than openly hateful. That respectable mask helps the group operate in a country where many communities still carry scars from the war.

## Connections

- [[Goblinblood Wars]] created the trauma and prejudice that feed the organization.
- [[Chitterwood]] and its goblin tribes remain the focus of the Slayers' hostility.
