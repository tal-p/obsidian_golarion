The Hellbreakers League is a rebel organization formed to free [[Isger]] from [[Cheliax]]. It has become the forefront of Isgeri dissidence after the [[Godsrain]].

## History

The League's cause surged when it discovered a [[Warshard]], a fragment of the dead god [[Gorum]], and turned it into a rallying symbol. That discovery drew the attention of Her Infernal Majestrix Abrogail Thrune II, who seeks such divine remnants for herself. Isgeri officials suspected the League was hiding warshards in [[Breachill]] or nearby [[Citadel Altaerein]], prompting the [[Order of the Rack]] occupation.

## Purpose & Beliefs

The Hellbreakers oppose [[House Thrune]]'s control over Isger and channel the growing belief that Cheliax can be overthrown. [[Milani]] is closely associated with their revolutionary ideals.

## Structure & Influence

Alceo Demetrias and Phoebe Demetrias are named as the League's rebellious leaders. The League's covert base is concentrated in [[Saringallow]], where a two-story house legally owned by the hobgoblin Rhokrag serves as headquarters and lists Phoebe and Alceo as tenants. The trio's regular consecration of the property has won neighbors' goodwill and silence.

An abandoned warehouse retained as the Beacon Candles Workshop serves as a decoy and source of funds. [[Elleri Smalltoes]] encodes messages through the candles' colors, shapes, and carved patterns. Other quiet supporters provide disguises, weapons, food, treatment, dockside advice, and second chances, while [[Jordis Serelli]]'s city watch remains hostile to insurgency.

After the [[Battle of Hellknight Hill]], the League emerged from nearby mountain caves to treat with Andoren forces, who promised aid in return for a powerful warshard taken back to [[Andoran]].

## Connections

- [[Breachill]] was occupied by Hellknights after rumors that League members were hidden there.
- [[Battle of Hellknight Hill]] grew out of Cheliax's effort to suppress the League.
- [[Godsrain]] gave the League both symbolic momentum and the Warshard that made it a Chelaxian target.
- [[Saringallow]] conceals the League's headquarters and a broad network of practical sympathizers.
- [[Elleri Smalltoes]] operates its decoy candle workshop and coded communications.
