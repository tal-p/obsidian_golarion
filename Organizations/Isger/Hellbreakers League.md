The Hellbreakers League is a rebel organization formed to free [[Isger]] from [[Cheliax]]. It has become the forefront of Isgeri dissidence after the [[Godsrain]].

## History

The League's cause surged when it discovered a [[Warshard]], a fragment of the dead god [[Gorum]], and turned it into a rallying symbol. That discovery drew the attention of Her Infernal Majestrix Abrogail Thrune II, who seeks such divine remnants for herself. Isgeri officials suspected the League was hiding warshards in [[Breachill]] or nearby [[Citadel Altaerein]], prompting the [[Order of the Rack]] occupation.

## Purpose & Beliefs

The Hellbreakers oppose [[House Thrune]]'s control over Isger and channel the growing belief that Cheliax can be overthrown. [[Milani]] is closely associated with their revolutionary ideals.

## Structure & Influence

Alceo Demetrias and Phoebe Demetrias are named as the League's rebellious leaders. Activity is especially common in [[Saringallow]], leading many to believe the city may be the League's headquarters. After the [[Battle of Hellknight Hill]], the League emerged from nearby mountain caves to treat with Andoren forces, who promised aid in return for a powerful warshard taken back to [[Andoran]].

## Connections

- [[Breachill]] was occupied by Hellknights after rumors that League members were hidden there.
- [[Battle of Hellknight Hill]] grew out of Cheliax's effort to suppress the League.
- [[Godsrain]] gave the League both symbolic momentum and the Warshard that made it a Chelaxian target.
