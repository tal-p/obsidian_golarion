The Savoring Society is an exclusive [[Absalom]] club whose gilded gatherings conceal a cult of [[Urgathoa]]. Its wealthy members treat gluttony, luxury, and the exploitation enabled by privilege as their rightful rewards.

## Purpose & Beliefs

The Society teaches that great wealth should purchase freedom from every restraint. Members boast of tasting every kind of living creature and drinking impossibly old wines, while revering Urgathoa as the patron who ensures their pleasures never end. Their creed ultimately presents the poor as something the rich must consume to enjoy privilege fully.

## Structure & Influence

Membership is drawn from Absalom's richest politicians, merchants, and nobles. Opulent gatherings can continue for weeks because the Society requires members not merely to indulge but to savor each extravagance without concern for time. Recruiters identify powerful prospects, feed their existing vices, and gradually direct their gratitude toward Urgathoa.

## Connections

- [[Absalom]] provides the wealth, influence, and anonymity behind the Society's public facade.
- [[Urgathoa]] sanctifies the excess and self-interest at the center of its gatherings.
