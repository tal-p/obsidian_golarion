The Order of the Rack is a Chelaxian Hellknight order deployed to [[Breachill]] at [[Hedvend VI]]'s request in Abadius 4725 AR. Abrogail Thrune II sent loyalist Rack Hellknights to root out treason and recover any warshards hidden by the [[Hellbreakers League]].

## History

By Gozran 4725 AR, the order occupied Breachill, placed most citizens under house arrest, and carried out public executions without finding League members. In Sarenith 4725 AR, they captured and publicly executed General Reginald Cormoth after he arrived with Andoren diplomats to request an end to the occupation.

Chelaxian supplies later allowed the order to fortify [[Citadel Altaerein]] with infernal wards and a conjured diabolical dragon. In Rova 4725 AR, Andoren forces retook Breachill and the citadel during the [[Battle of Hellknight Hill]]. Some Rack Hellknights were captured, while many others retreated toward [[Cheliax]].

## Connections
