The Order of the Nail is a Hellknight order founded in 4638 AR. Soon after its founding, it constructed [[Citadel Altaerein]] near [[Breachill]] in [[Isger]], leaving the citadel as its major Isgeri legacy.

## History

Citadel Altaerein served as the order's Isgeri base until the order moved to [[Korvosa]] in 4682 AR. A small Hellknight presence remained at the citadel for decades, but it was fully abandoned in 4711 AR.
