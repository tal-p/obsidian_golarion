Vordia is an Isgeri town rebuilt with significant help from the [[Sisterhood of the Golden Erinys]] and the [[Church of Asmodeus]] after the [[Goblinblood Wars]]. It is the site of one of the first orphanages built by the sisterhood after the war.

## People & Culture

Because the sisters cared for Vordia after the war, many of its citizens are grateful to [[Cheliax]] and loyal to Chelaxian rule.
