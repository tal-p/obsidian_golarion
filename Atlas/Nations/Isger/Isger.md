Isger is a temperate vassal state of [[Cheliax]] poised between revolt and deeper subjugation. Its people have lived for centuries under outside rule, first by [[Taldor]] and later by Cheliax, and recent turmoil has left many Isgeri close to open rebellion against [[House Thrune]].

The country is still healing from the [[Goblinblood Wars]], and another war could leave it vulnerable to conquest or exploitation. The [[Godsrain]] and the rise of the [[Hellbreakers League]] have made that danger immediate.

Its major resources include livestock, hides, lumber, and textiles.

## Geography

Isger is bounded by the [[River Keld]] and [[Aspodell Mountains]] to the south, the [[Menador Mountains]] to the north, and the [[Five Kings Mountains]] to the east. It borders [[Cheliax]], [[Andoran]], [[Druma]], and [[Molthune]], though direct travel to Molthune requires passage through the northern mountains.

Most of the country is plains and hills. The [[Chitterwood]] is its largest forest, and the [[Conerica River]] is a major trade corridor through the center of the nation and along much of its western border. The Conerica is fed by the North Conerica River, which joins the Summerun River at [[Elidir]].

## History

### Isgeri Tribes and Foreign Rule

Before conquest, eleven Kellid tribes known collectively as the Isgeri lived between the Menador and Five Kings Mountains. They hunted, fished, traded, and held regular moots near the river confluence where [[Elidir]] later rose.

In 2133 AR, Taldor's Seventh Army of Exploration defeated the indigenous Isgeri and founded Isger as a Taldan province. The new country was named Isger after the defeated people, but the land remained under Taldan control for nearly 2,000 years. After Cheliax declared independence from [[Taldor]] in the Even-Tongued Conquest of 4081 AR, Isger was annexed and became a Chelaxian vassal state.

### Goblinblood Wars and Aftermath

Chelaxian expansion into the [[Chitterwood]] sparked the [[Goblinblood Wars]] in 4697 AR. Hobgoblin warlords mobilized goblin tribes, some willing and some forced, and their armies devastated Isger until an alliance of [[Hellknights]], Druma's Mercenary League, and Andoran's Eagle Knights burned back the Chitterwood and halted the invasion.

After the war, Isger's army was so badly damaged that Hedvend VI ordered his remaining forces to focus on vital trade routes and leave much of the countryside to fend for itself. The Church of [[Asmodeus]] and [[House Thrune]] offered aid with strings attached. Asmodean priests cared for the wounded in exchange for future favors, and the [[Sisterhood of the Golden Erinys]] raised orphaned children in newly built orphanages while indoctrinating them into Asmodean faith.

## Government & Military

Isger is ruled by [[Hedvend VI]], a Chelaxian-appointed steward based in [[Elidir]]. His government oversees the nation and its trade, but the capital's elites largely neglect settlements outside Elidir. Most towns and villages rely on sheriffs, mayors, guards, and local councils to manage daily civic life.

Cheliax requires Isger to maintain a standing army to protect trade routes and borders but provides little money, materiel, or training. The [[Isgeri Army]] is made up of volunteer recruits and patrols the Conerica River as well as the Druman and Andoren borders. Since the [[Battle of Hellknight Hill]], many soldiers have been pulled back to defend Elidir.

## People & Culture

Most Isgeri are humans of Taldan or Kellid descent. Goblins are the next largest population, especially in the [[Chitterwood]], and gnomes are common thanks in part to [[Umok]]. Other peoples are concentrated mostly in riverside settlements.

Isger's society is divided between wealthy Elidirans and rural communities still rebuilding after war. Countryside Isgeri tend to be stubborn, practical, and deeply communal; neighbors help one another because survival demands it. Simple foods such as porridge, roast chicken, and salads are common, and many rural Isgeri dislike heavily spiced meals.

Practical clothing matters more than fashion. Hats protect against sun and wind, scarves guard against dust and cold, and subtle symbols such as flowers, skulls, and dyed nails carry local meaning.

Religious life is similarly divided. [[Asmodeus]] is the official patron under Chelaxian rule, [[Abadar]] has a strong following, [[Erastil]] is popular in the countryside, [[Milani]] has grown among rebels, and [[Urgathoa]]'s influence spreads through [[Illcayna Alonnor]]. In [[Elidir]], an old temple of [[Nocticula]] has become a point of tension between her older demonic faithful and newer Redeemer Queen converts.

## Current Affairs

The [[Godsrain]] emboldened Isgeri rebels by proving that even divine powers can fall. The [[Hellbreakers League]] found a [[Warshard]] of [[Gorum]] and turned it into a revolutionary symbol, drawing the attention of Her Infernal Majestrix Abrogail Thrune II.

After the Godsrain, Isgeri officials suspected the Hellbreakers were hiding warshards in [[Breachill]] or nearby [[Citadel Altaerein]]. Cheliax sent [[Order of the Rack]] Hellknights to root out the Hellbreakers and reclaim any warshards they found. Their occupation and execution of Andoren envoy Reginald Cormoth escalated into the [[Battle of Hellknight Hill]], a clash some consider the first shot in a possible Cheliax-Andoran war.

As of Rova 4725 AR, Breachill and Citadel Altaerein have been retaken by Andoren forces, surviving Order of the Rack Hellknights are prisoners or retreating toward Cheliax, and the Hellbreakers have emerged from nearby mountain caves to negotiate with Andoran.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Hedvend VI]]: Chelaxian-appointed steward of Isger, widely seen as loyal to Cheliax and negligent toward the countryside.
- [[Hellbreakers League]]: Rebel organization seeking to free Isger from Chelaxian rule.
- [[Isgeri Army]]: Undersupported volunteer army charged with protecting Isger's routes and borders.
- [[Sisterhood of the Golden Erinys]]: Asmodean charitable order that runs orphanages and shapes rural loyalties.
- [[Illcayna Alonnor]]: Daughter of Urgathoa building undead power from [[Finder's Gulch]].
- [[Lady Kaltessa Iyis]]: Priestess of Mammon who watches Isger from the [[Iron Rose]].
- [[Mama Grimclaw]]: Iron hag who kidnaps children and hides them in [[Good Home]].
- [[Isgeri Slayers]]: Anti-goblin faction led by Varskand Gilwrad.

## Key Locations

- [[Elidir]]: Capital city and trade hub at the Summerun and North Conerica rivers.
- [[Chitterwood]]: Goblin-haunted forest scarred by the Goblinblood Wars.
- [[Breachill]]: Small town recently occupied by Hellknights and tied to the Battle of Hellknight Hill.
- [[Citadel Altaerein]]: Former Order of the Nail fortress near Breachill whose ownership remains uncertain.
- [[Citadel Dinyar]]: Headquarters of the [[Order of the Godclaw]] in the Aspodell Mountains.
- [[Saringallow]]: Rebellious city with deep resentment toward Chelaxian nobility.
- [[Logas]]: Former capital still marked by siege and anti-goblin distrust.
- [[Finder's Gulch]]: Haunted quarry town claimed by [[Illcayna Alonnor]].
- [[Good Home]]: Hidden town controlled by [[Mama Grimclaw]].
- [[Iron Rose]]: Devil-built fortress of [[Lady Kaltessa Iyis]].
