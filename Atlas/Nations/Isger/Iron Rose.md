The Iron Rose is the fortress-palace of [[Lady Kaltessa Iyis]] in a lush valley near the Druman border. Its elaborate stone arches display clear Chelaxian influence.

## History

Centuries ago, Kaltessa claimed the valley and conscripted dozens of devils to build the palace she calls home.

## Features

The Iron Rose is rumored to contain enough gold to buy entire nations, though no outsider is known to have entered and confirmed the stories.
