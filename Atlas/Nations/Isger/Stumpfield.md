Stumpfield is a young artists' colony on land once cleared from the northern [[Chitterwood]], founded to build fellowship between goblin and human creators. Its wagon-built homes, semi-permanent tents, and exuberant communal art give it the atmosphere of a traveling festival.

## History

The site was once part of the Chitterwood, which the [[Isgeri Army]] cut and burned during the [[Goblinblood Wars]] to deny its goblin enemies cover. [[Fernsby]] later cleared another section for lumber. The remaining field of stumps became Stumpfield when [[Erk Oilfingers]] and [[Jett Saldath]] met while separately depicting the same twisted tree and formed a fast friendship. They founded the colony together eighteen months before the Hellbreakers' visit.

## People & Culture

Stumpfield welcomes artists of every kind, though its several dozen residents are predominantly goblin and human. The community is supportive, unconventional, and largely detached from politics beyond its borders. Once each month, every resident contributes to a single work of cooperative art. Finished communal pieces stand throughout the settlement, chaotic in composition but often compelling. Celebrations feature shared wine and improvised communal stew.

## Current Affairs

Stumpfield relies on nearby [[Fernsby]] for food and practical goods. Their already strained relationship nearly broke down after goblin-headed snakes repeatedly vandalized Fernsby's village green and allowed themselves to be mistaken for Stumpfield goblins. The attacks were orchestrated by [[Hissbite]], who resented the colony's friendship between humans and goblins. Exposing the deception preserved trade between the two settlements.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Erk Oilfingers]] - Visionary goblin painter and cofounder who welcomes visitors into the colony's artistic life.
- [[Jett Saldath]] - Focused human sculptor and cofounder of Stumpfield.
- [[Hissbite]] - Gobmob snake whose jealousy of Stumpfield inspired a campaign to turn Fernsby against it.

## Key Locations

- Town center - Gathering place for monthly cooperative art projects and the celebrations that follow them.
