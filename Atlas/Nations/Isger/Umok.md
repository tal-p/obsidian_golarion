Umok is a centuries-old gnome village between the [[Chitterwood]] and the [[Aspodell Mountains]]. Founded as a haven for gnomes seeking harmony with the forest, it is known for its strict prohibition on fire, its animal sentries, and its Isgeri boarhounds.

## History

Competition over game and timber repeatedly brought Umok into conflict with nearby goblin camps. During the [[Goblinblood Wars]], hobgoblins attempted to burn the village, but the visiting Caydenite champion [[Loriwyse Marblemug]] rallied its defenders and saved the community. Umok thereafter outlawed fire within 500 yards of its perimeter. Banishment is the usual punishment, though some goblin offenders have been executed.

## Features

Signs in Common and Gnomish mark Umok's fire-free boundary. Residents use alchemy and magic for cooking, warmth, and illumination; colorful magical orbs light streets lined with ivy-covered, peaked-roof cottages.

Domesticated woodland animals patrol the village and surrounding forest. Small animals carry warnings back to town, while larger guardians such as wolves may confront travelers bearing open flames. Deer and badgers wander freely through the streets alongside prized Isgeri boarhounds, a local breed renowned for hunting extraplanar quarry.

## Current Affairs

Umok is remote enough that most residents are neutral toward [[Cheliax]] and reluctant to attract imperial attention through rebellion. A recent series of fires southwest of town inflamed old suspicions of goblins, especially after the badly burned goblin forager [[Grizzo]] collapsed in the village. The true culprits were two hell hounds summoned in the nearby Charred Wood during a ritual performed by [[Viro Ahala]] and members of the [[Sisterhood of the Golden Erinys]]. Their destruction exonerated Grizzo.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Ioan Marblemug]] — Captain of the guard and descendant of Umok's historic champion; initially believed Grizzo responsible for the fires.
- [[Loriwyse Marblemug]] — Champion of [[Cayden Cailean]] who saved Umok during the Goblinblood Wars.
- [[Grizzo]] — Reclusive goblin forager burned by the hell hounds responsible for the recent fires.

## Key Locations

- **Charred Wood** — A roughly hundred-foot circle of burned forest southwest of Umok, created when Viro Ahala and the sisters summoned two hell hounds.
