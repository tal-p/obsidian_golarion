Umok is a centuries-old gnome village in [[Isger]]. It is one reason gnomes are unusually common in the country.

## Features

Because of past conflicts with fire-loving goblins from the [[Chitterwood]], Umok has outlawed fire within 500 yards of its buildings. Residents rely on alchemy and magic for heat and light.
