Frog Bog is the colloquial name for the swampy eastern [[Chitterwood]], where the [[Mudrub tribe]] lives among floodwaters, mud huts, frogs, snakes, and birds.

## Features

Frog Bog is consistently soggy in the warm months and dries out during winter. It is known for a large frog population, and the frogs are said to grow larger each year, which locals take as a sign that the Chitterwood is healing. Rumors claim that a cart-sized frog lives deep in the dirt, and many Mudrub goblins have tried to find it.

Mudrub homes are tall mud huts built to withstand flooding. Rock paths serve as stepping stones when waters rise.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Mudrub tribe]]: Swamp-dwelling goblins who hide in muck, hunt frogs by hand, and live in flood-resistant mud huts.
