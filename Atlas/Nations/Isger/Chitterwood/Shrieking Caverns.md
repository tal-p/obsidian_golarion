Shrieking Caverns is the bat-filled cave system where the [[People of the Swarm]] live beneath the [[Chitterwood]]. The caverns are named for the cries of thousands of bats swarming through the caves.

## Features

After the [[Goblinblood Wars]], traumatized Swarm goblins found a massive bat colony here and took its noise and chaos as a sign of safety. The caverns contain threats such as cave bears, but an underground river also provides fish and supports lichen. High ceilings, low pits, humidity, and darkness make the caves dangerous, while nooks and crannies give goblins elevated sleeping places away from floodwaters and predators.

Fires can be difficult to keep lit in the damp caves, so residents sometimes rely on glowing worms and fungi for light.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[People of the Swarm]]: Large goblin tribe that treats the bats of the caverns as kindred spirits.
