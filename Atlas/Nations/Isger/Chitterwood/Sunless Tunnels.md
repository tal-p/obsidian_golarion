The Sunless Tunnels are deep southern caverns beneath the [[Chitterwood]] and the home of the [[Hookback tribe]]. They are dark, ore-rich, and dangerous even to the goblins who live there.

## Features

Hookback goblins retreated into the southern caves after the [[Goblinblood Wars]] and made the tunnels the center of their postwar culture. The tunnels are rich in valuable metal ores, which the tribe uses for its iconic piercings and specialized smelting traditions despite its restrained, ceremonial use of fire.

Something unknown lives in the deepest tunnels. Hookback goblins know where its heavy breathing can be heard and avoid those passages.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Hookback tribe]]: Goblin tribe that lives in the tunnels and uses their ores for ceremonial piercings.
