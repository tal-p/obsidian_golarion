Barking Caverns is one of the most expansive cave systems beneath the [[Chitterwood]] and the home of the [[Spine Threshers]]. Its name comes from the barks of the tribe's mounts echoing through the tunnels.

## Features

The cave system became both refuge and training ground after the [[Goblinblood Wars]], when surviving Spine Threshers needed to preserve their riders, mounts, and traditions. Wide chambers provide room to run, ride, and train, while smaller tunnels are used for racing and practice. Squeezing through narrow passages is a way for riders to prove their worth.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Spine Threshers]]: Goblin tribe that lives, trains, races, and raises mounts in the caverns.
