Stagfall Caves are ancient caves in the [[Chitterwood]] that have sheltered many creatures over the ages. Deep within them lies a dormant nexus of primal power.

## Features

The caves long predate the recent troubles in Isger and have served as home and shelter for many creatures since ancient days. Their most important known feature is a nexus of primal power hidden deep inside, dormant for centuries until the events surrounding the Hellbreakers campaign.
