The Chitterwood is Isger's largest forest and the home of many goblin tribes. It is old-growth woodland, though much reduced since the [[Goblinblood Wars]], and far more dangerous creatures than goblins are said to lurk among its trees.

## Geography

The forest lies in southern [[Isger]] near the [[Conerica River]], the [[Aspodell Mountains]], and [[Logas]]. Its old hardwoods were badly reduced by wartime fires, leaving burned margins, recovering growth, swampy eastern reaches, and extensive caverns below the surviving woods. A lesser trade route cuts through the forest and is favored by smugglers, bandits, and travelers who want to avoid Isger's better-patrolled roads.

The forest is damaged but not dead. Birds, bears, boars, silk moths, mulberries, frogs, and other ordinary life still survive among the trees and wetlands, while darker things linger where war and fire scarred the land.

### Caverns

The caves beneath the Chitterwood became the refuge of many surviving goblins after the Goblinblood Wars. They are broad enough in places to shelter whole tribes, but they are harsh homes: light is scarce, humidity is high, food is limited, and cave bears, bats, rats, fish, frogs, fungi, insects, newts, and lichen make up much of the underground ecology.

Goblins are unusually well suited to this life thanks to darkvision, scavenging habits, and hardy digestion. Rats are stewed, bats are pickled, and fungi and insects form much of the everyday diet. Younger goblins sometimes risk trips aboveground for food, trophies, or curiosity, while elders scarred by the war often discourage such ventures.

## History

Before the war, many Chitterwood goblins were reclusive forest dwellers whose contact with non-goblinoids was often limited to scavenging, theft, and occasional ambushes of travelers. That isolation changed in 4697 AR, when hobgoblin commanders and goblin armies launched the attacks that began the Goblinblood Wars.

Hobgoblin warlords used the forest's goblin tribes to form a massive army, drawing in both volunteers angry at human mistreatment and conscripts forced into the conflict. The war lasted four years and devastated settlements from [[Varisia]] to [[Taldor]].

The war ended only after an alliance of [[Hellknights]], Druma's Mercenary League, and Andoran's Eagle Knights set the forest's edges aflame. The Chitterwood survived, but its size was greatly reduced. Many goblins hid in caves during the burning and later returned to reclaim the woods.

For the goblin tribes, the war was nearly annihilating. They were dominated by hobgoblin commanders, hunted afterward by the [[Isgeri Slayers]], and forced underground with most of their warriors dead. Six generations later, Chitterwood goblin cultures are still shaped by displacement, fear, survival, and memories of hobgoblin cruelty.

## People & Culture

Most Chitterwood goblins live in or beneath the forest, shaped by six generations of survival after the Goblinblood Wars. Hundreds of goblinoid cultures are said to dwell here. Many share older goblin habits such as fear of dogs and horses, a love of song and fire, and wariness of writing, but their postwar lives have pushed them in different directions.

### Goblin Tribes and Movements

Chitterwood goblin groups adapted to the war's aftermath in sharply different ways. Some turned old torment into identity, some preserved resistance through songs and fire, some became deeper cavern-dwellers, and others built cultures around stealth, mounted combat, invention, or trade along the forest edge.

Not all goblins remained in the caverns. The [[Crookedtoes]] became travelers and adventurers, reaching as far as [[Absalom]] and aiding the [[Knights of Lastwall]], while the [[Dragondweller tribe]] helped human nations and earned protection from allies.

### Hobgoblins and Other Residents

Since the founding of [[Oprak]] in 4717 AR, many hobgoblins have a homeland elsewhere, but a few still hide in the Chitterwood's caverns and wait for a useful moment to reveal themselves. Local goblins rarely serve them willingly now, though some remain under hobgoblin control.

Most non-goblinoids avoid living in the Chitterwood, which makes it useful to smugglers, bandits, fugitives, and others who value seclusion. [[Stumpfield]] and [[Fernsby]] are the best-known nearby humanoid settlements, with Stumpfield notable for its mixed goblinoid and non-goblinoid population.

## Current Affairs

Relations between Chitterwood goblins and nearby human settlements remain tense, especially around communities still scarred by the war such as [[Logas]] and [[Gillamoor]]. Some leaders cautiously seek diplomacy with non-goblins, partly because undead and other threats increasingly trouble the countryside, but the Isgeri Slayers, Cinder Seekers, and hidden hobgoblin holdouts all make peace more fragile.

The woods remain dangerous beyond politics. Ghosts and haunts are common where the Goblinblood Wars spilled blood. Polluted fey spirits are said to have been twisted by fire and blood-soaked soil. Rumors speak of the [[Goblinblood Beast]], a haunt or monster born from wartime bloodshed that causes visions of war and burning, as well as dragons, a forest dragon attempting to claim part of the Chitterwood, the lost hoard of [[Jurinex]], and an ancient beast of living shadow.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Brakknap]] - Goblin elder working to repair relations between goblinoids and humanoids.
- [[Bulbhead tribe]] - Gourd-helmeted goblins of the Burnt Ends who trade with outsiders and feud with the [[Isgeri Slayers]].
- [[Cinder Seekers]] - Goblin movement dedicated to preventing future hobgoblin domination through memory, song, and fire.
- [[Hookback tribe]] - Deep-cavern goblins whose piercings preserve a painful history of survival.
- [[Mudrub tribe]] - Swamp-dwelling goblins of the eastern Chitterwood who cover themselves in mud and reject written marks.
- [[People of the Swarm]] - Cavern goblins known for songs and old grudges against hobgoblin commanders.
- [[Spine Threshers]] - Martial goblin tribe associated with mounted training in the Barking Caverns.
- [[True Hoard tribe]] - Scavengers, trap makers, alchemists, and inventors who prize anything useful, shiny, or strange.
- [[Isgeri Slayers]] - Anti-goblin faction that hunts goblinoids in and around the Chitterwood.

## Key Locations

- [[Barking Caverns]] - Expansive cave system used by the Spine Threshers to run, ride, race, and train mounts.
- [[Big Fire]] - Bonfire deep in the woods tended by the Cinder Seekers and treated by them as sacred.
- [[Burnt Ends]] - Recovering forest edge where the Bulbhead tribe farms gourds and vegetables in once-charred soil.
- [[Frog Bog]] - Swampy eastern Chitterwood claimed by the Mudrub tribe and known for its frogs.
- [[Goldenmouth]] - Rumored lost hoard of the dragon [[Jurinex]], supposedly hidden after fire destroyed the landmarks to it.
- [[Logas]] - Former capital and riverside city near the forest.
- [[Gillamoor]] - Settlement on the forest's edge that survived goblin attacks before falling to undeath.
- [[Shrieking Caverns]] - Cave system of the People of the Swarm, named for its huge bat colonies.
- [[Stagfall Caves]] - Ancient caves containing a dormant nexus of primal power.
- [[Stumpfield]] - Artist colony near the woods with both goblinoid and non-goblinoid residents.
- [[Sunless Tunnels]] - Ore-rich southern caves of the Hookback tribe, with deeper tunnels avoided even by the goblins.
- [[Umok]] - Gnome village shaped by past conflict with fire-loving goblins from the Chitterwood.
