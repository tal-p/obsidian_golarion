The Burnt Ends are the recovering edges of the [[Chitterwood]] where the [[Bulbhead tribe]] makes its home. The name refers to land scarred by the fires that ended the [[Goblinblood Wars]].

## Features

The area was left charred when allied forces burned back the Chitterwood, but younger trees and new growth have slowly reclaimed the damaged margins. The Burnt Ends have less cover than most goblins prefer, but their once-burned soil supports Bulbhead gardens of gourds, vegetables, and other crops. Bulbheads also use gourd patches to mark places worth returning to.

Bulbhead goblins move through the Burnt Ends in temporary, camouflaged camps. Their proximity to the forest edge makes them more likely than many Chitterwood goblins to trade with outsiders.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Bulbhead tribe]]: Goblins who farm gourds and vegetables in the recovering forest edge.
- [[Isgeri Slayers]]: Enemies of the Bulbheads who sometimes operate near the Chitterwood.
