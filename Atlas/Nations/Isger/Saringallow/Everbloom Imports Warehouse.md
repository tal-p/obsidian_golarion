Everbloom Imports Warehouse is the [[Hellbreakers League]]'s concealed dockside supply depot in [[Saringallow]]. Operating under the name of a fictitious trading company, it receives weapons, armor, ammunition, food, and other provisions acquired by League agents across [[Isger]].

## History

The League established Everbloom Imports because [[Hellbreakers' Headquarters]] lacked space for its growing stockpile. Both the company's name and its work quietly invoke [[Milani]], whose faith associates rebellion against tyranny with blooming roses.

## Features

The warehouse stands in partial isolation at the eastern end of Dockside Row, opposite the [[Conerica Docks]]. It consists of a square main building with broad wagon doors and a smaller eastern annex. The annex holds old crates, forged bills of lading, and packing materials retained for later smuggling operations; the main floor stores the League's active supplies.

Agents posing as merchants deliver goods by river, unload them under the Everbloom name, and return to missions elsewhere. [[Gertraud Mildenhall]] inventories incoming cargo and folds captured or donated equipment into the stockpile.

## Current Affairs

A supposed supply shipment from [[Logas]] was infiltrated by Chelaxian loyalists posing as Hellbreakers. They deceived a stevedore into revealing the warehouse, forced their way inside, and captured Gertraud in hopes of collecting [[Hedvend's Bounty on Dissidents]] from additional agents. The loyalists were defeated before they could destroy the existing stores, though the expected Logas shipment proved to be a ruse.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Hellbreakers League]] - Owns the warehouse through a fictitious import company and uses it to provision field operations.
- [[Gertraud Mildenhall]] - Junior Hellbreaker responsible for receiving and inventorying supplies.
- [[Xerra Bluebottle]] - League quartermaster who coordinates the wider effort to arm and provision the rebellion.
