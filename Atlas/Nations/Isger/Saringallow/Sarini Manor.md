Sarini Manor is the half-derelict estate of [[House Sarini]] and the oldest surviving structure in [[Saringallow]]. The servants' revolt against the house founded the city, while a much later outbreak of demonic corruption at the abandoned property renewed its reputation as the source of Saringallow's oldest fears.

## History

The minor Chelaxian [[House Sarini]] founded the garden estate of Sarini's Hollow at the ancient Crosspaths, where guests could amuse themselves with amateur archaeology. One excavation uncovered a beautiful pristine glaive that was sent to [[Elidir]]. The family practiced diabolism long before [[House Thrune]] rose to power, and several village youths died in their rituals.

After servants repeatedly vanished under mysterious circumstances, the household's other servants and local residents rebelled and hanged every Sarini they could find. Their fall ended the estate economy and began Saringallow's self-rule, but also triggered a decade of witch hunts, dogma, and paranoia. Sarini's Hollow became Sarini Gallows and, over time, [[Saringallow]].

Centuries later, mysterious events at the abandoned estate exposed demonic corruption and demon bile. Saringallow expanded its security after the crisis and began slowly reassessing its fear of magic.

## Features

The estate remains partly ruined but still looms over local identity. A path called the Noble Folly leads toward it, and attempting the climb remains a traditional dare among the city's children despite the nearby watch barracks.

## Current Affairs

[[Old Hawk]] insists that the Sarinis will one day descend upon Saringallow to reclaim their property. Younger residents humor him, but many older residents are weary of the fear his warnings keep alive.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[House Sarini]]: Chelaxian noble family whose suspected abuses and lynching led to Saringallow's founding.
- [[Old Hawk]]: Superstitious elder preparing for the family's imagined return.
