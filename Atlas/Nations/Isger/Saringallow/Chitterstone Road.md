Chitterstone Road is the unofficial name of [[Saringallow]]'s vibrant goblin and hobgoblin neighborhood, founded by the [[Stonechewer tribe]] after residents refused to rent homes to them. Its success embodies the city's recent effort to prevent renewed conflict with the [[Chitterwood]] by welcoming its peoples.

## Features

The neighborhood has ordinary businesses serving daily needs, with many goods especially popular among goblinoids. It is best known for Mashnom's Mercs and Mortar and its concentration of skilled masons, construction companies, and mercenary associations.

## Current Affairs

Mashnom, a mute female goblin union leader, guides the community. What began as an answer to housing discrimination has become an economically important enclave and a visible test of Saringallow's increasing openness.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Stonechewer tribe]]: Goblin and hobgoblin clan that built the neighborhood.
- Mashnom: Union leader who organized the tribe's building and mercenary work.
