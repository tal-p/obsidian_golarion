The Hellbreakers' Headquarters is a concealed safe house on the eastern side of [[Saringallow]] and the operational heart of the [[Hellbreakers League]]. Its ordinary domestic appearance hides a purpose-built basement where the rebels plan missions, train recruits, and coordinate their campaign against [[Cheliax]].

## History

The house once belonged to the [[Vazarros]], a family whose alchemist patriarch became corrupted by demonic influence. The Hellbreakers helped the family relocate, purchased the property cheaply, and excavated a large basement beneath it.

The house is legally owned by [[Rhokrag]], with League founders [[Alceo Demetrias]] and [[Phoebe Demetrias]] listed as tenants. Their repeated consecration of the property earned the neighbors' goodwill and protective silence.

## Features

The ground floor resembles a comfortable private home, with a central dining table and a hearth along the eastern wall. A locked wooden door opens onto stairs into the basement. Visitors identify themselves at the front door as "friends of Rose," invoking [[Milani]] and her Refuge of the Red Rose; an initiated doorkeeper replies that any friends of Rose can find refuge there.

The broad basement contains crowded tables, documents, ledgers, and maps of [[Isger]]. A cleared side room serves as a training area, while a smaller alcove contains a magically heated iron stove that produces light and warmth without smoke. Agents occasionally sleep upstairs, but most maintain homes elsewhere in Saringallow or use friendly inns such as [[The Fiend's Finale]].

## Current Affairs

After the [[Battle of Hellknight Hill]], Alceo and Phoebe traveled to [[Elidir]] to oppose [[Hedvend's Bounty on Dissidents]] through sympathetic government officials. Rhokrag directed field missions from the safe house, [[Maire Phelan]] managed recruitment and public relations, and [[Xerra Bluebottle]] oversaw provisions. [[Ethran Howell]] joined the League here as a soldier and tactician responsible for training new recruits.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Hellbreakers League]] - Maintains the house as its principal covert command center.
- [[Rhokrag]] - Legal owner and field leader who assigns missions while the founders operate elsewhere.
- [[Maire Phelan]] - Recruiter, storyteller, and public-relations lieutenant who helps receive new agents.
- [[Xerra Bluebottle]] - Quartermaster responsible for keeping the headquarters supplied and armed.
- [[Alceo Demetrias]] and [[Phoebe Demetrias]] - League founders legally presented as the property's tenants.
