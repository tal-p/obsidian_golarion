Saringallow is a young riverside city in central [[Isger]], known for surviving the [[Goblinblood Wars]], distrusting aristocracy and entrenched power, and quietly harboring the [[Hellbreakers League]]. Its old walls, strategic position on the [[Conerica River]], and tradition of judging people by their conduct in a crisis have made it both a refuge and a natural stage for rebellion as another war approaches.

## Geography

Saringallow stands near Isger's heart, where the ancient Crosspaths once gathered the peoples living between the [[Aspodell Mountains|Aspodell]] and [[Menador Mountains]]. River traffic and the Conerica Highway connect it to the rest of the country, including nearby [[Swiftrun]], with which it enjoys especially good relations.

More than 2,500 people live within the walls and about 800 in the surrounding countryside, where some communities approach the size of small towns. The city is outgrowing its historic plan: unregulated housing and slums have gathered along the north wall, while its streets, buildings, and prospective districts have yet to be formally named or numbered.

## History

### Crosspaths and House Sarini

Long before Isger's founding, the site was called the Crosspaths and served as a regular meeting place where local peoples reaffirmed their alliances. [[House Sarini]] chose it for an estate partly so guests could pursue amateur archaeology. Excavators uncovered a beautiful, pristine glaive that was sent to [[Elidir]], where it has remained.

The settlement first grew as Sarini's Hollow, a garden estate and dependent village of the minor Chelaxian [[House Sarini]]. The Sarinis practiced diabolism long before [[House Thrune]] came to power, and their rituals killed several local youths. After servants repeatedly vanished, the household's servants and other residents rebelled and hanged every Sarini they could find. Sarini's Hollow became Sarini Gallows and, eventually, Saringallow.

The settlement's first decade of self-rule descended into witch hunts, dogma, and intense paranoia. Many ancestors of its rural families risked the wilderness rather than remain inside the walls. Without the manor economy or goods arriving for its masters, the new town nearly collapsed until river traffic made it a rest stop for north-south travelers; road trade followed.

### Goblinblood Wars

Growth remained slow for roughly two centuries and depended heavily on residents who left to seek fortune and returned successful. Many came home empty-handed or never returned. The [[Goblinblood Wars]] transformed the town. Its historic ramparts, disparagingly called the Pauper's Roof, held while communities around it burned, drawing refugees and exhausted soldiers and giving Saringallow an early start on recovery.

The walls had been provisioned for only about 2,000 people. With no official resupply, the mayor limited foreign soldiers to one meal and one night's rest per week; the wounded could stay a fortnight but were then barred for a month. Locals defied the ordinance by claiming strangers as relatives, sheltering them at home, and risking expeditions beyond the walls to forage, fish, and hunt.

Local folklore holds that an Eagle Knight, a Hellknight, and a commander of Druma's Mercenary League discovered one another hiding under the same false identity in the same house. Recognizing their common purpose, they quietly coordinated troop movements despite their superiors' ideological refusal to cooperate. Their victories supposedly forced the higher commands into the alliance that saved Isger.

Few witnesses survived. [[Hedvend VI]] exploited the uncertainty to suggest he had secretly arranged the alliance, then threatened to divert reconstruction and protection when Saringallow objected. Burdened by refugees, the town stayed silent and allowed its role to fade behind its grim name and superstitious reputation.

### Demonic Corruption and Recovery

Saringallow later suffered a crisis of demonic corruption centered on [[Sarini Manor]]. Its recovery renewed growth and helped recast old social distance: many residents now reserve judgment not simply from fear, but because they believe conduct in a crisis reveals character more truly than appearances do.

## Government & Military

### Mayoral Government

Saringallow elects a mayor but remains subject to Elidir and Isger's steward. The office predates the Sarinis' deaths. Mayors originally served for life; after the first century, candidates began proposing terms of five to ten years so major infrastructure could be completed under one administration. Campaigning traditionally begins two years before a term ends.

[[Sandra Trinelli]] has less than a year remaining in her term. Her expansion of City Hall's staff and records prepared Saringallow for formal districts, named streets, and numbered buildings. It also let residents process deferrals when the steward imposed a new wartime tax that might otherwise have drained the city.

### The Old Book

Most law follows Isgeri standards, but Saringallow sometimes invokes the Old Book, reportedly derived from an ancient copy of the Abadaran *Manual of City-Building* whose title more literally means *Laws of Civilization-Building*. Supporters consider it friendlier to disenfranchised people and those living from the land; critics argue those same provisions are poorly suited to a growing city.

### City Watch

The city expanded its security after the demonic corruption crisis. [[Jordis Serelli]] commands the watch from a barracks inside the Noble Folly gate, leading nightly graveyard rounds and handling crimes, disturbances, and attempts by youths to climb the Noble Folly path. Jordis distrusts insurgents, including well-intentioned ones, as likely to cause greater harm, although they are no Chelaxian sympathizer.

## People & Culture

### Communities and Generations

Saringallow's three broad communities are descendants of the Sarinis' servants, immigrants who arrived before the Goblinblood Wars, and those who came afterward. Old servant families can often be recognized by surname and strict traditions of prayer and superstition. Laws once privileged them in land and home ownership, but most such discrimination has recently been removed.

Before the wars, Saringallow was overwhelmingly human. Halflings and gnomes were among the first new peoples to settle afterward; goblins and hobgoblins arrived only in recent years. Some early goblinoid settlers were paid to come because the city concluded that accepting the peoples of the [[Chitterwood]] was the surest way to prevent future conflict. Refugees came both because their homes were ruined and because Saringallow's proven defenses offered security.

Age often predicts outlook better than ancestry. Older residents tend to be reserved, stoic, hardworking, and deeply superstitious. Younger residents and recent immigrants are more direct, opportunistic, and open-minded, while children and adolescents are famously curious and mischievous. Nearly everyone despises nobility; even benevolent runaway aristocrats must prove themselves and have formed their own insular circle.

### Social Circles and Mobility

Closed social circles shape education, work, and friendship. Families provide most schooling, apprenticeships remain the main route to a career, and birth heavily determines class and status. Adventuring or a decorated military career are the most dependable paths to social mobility. Old sayings claim a person could spend an entire life here without meeting the neighbor who will one day lie beside them in the graveyard.

### Magic and Superstition

The [[Godsrain]] greatly softened the city's old fear of magic. Divine power had once seemed the most reliable or at least predictably moral kind, but witnessing a god's death persuaded many that a wielder's intent matters more than the source of their power. Anti-magic signs have come down and practitioners are more open, aided by the Magaambyan band Oasis Rain Showers and its willingness to explain magic.

Saringallow folk maintain a dense tradition of gestures, sayings, and tokens meant to answer every misfortune. The city presents a respectable face to outsiders, but internal disputes are growing. Older residents lament the loss of its rustic character, as do nearby fey angered by expanding settlement and by centuries without meaningful communion; their resentment may soon become open hostility.

### Holidays

Holidays bridge otherwise separate circles. Local observances include Nightgallop, a rural horse race listed on the first new moon of Desnus and also described as occurring on spring's first full moon; Succession, a summer-solstice torch-passing in which teachers approve their apprentices; Shearwalk, a week of chasing and shearing sheep; Oaksplit, when lumber is processed; Snowsow, when winter-wheat grain is blessed; Harvest Feast, a week of communal meals; and Loomgather, a local form of Shelyn's Crystalhue. Gallows Day on 13 Arodus commemorates the founding and has shifted from somber religious observance toward tourism.

The broader calendar includes Vault Day on 6 Abadius and 6 Sarenith, Steward's Day on 23 Abadius, Merrymead on 2 Calistril, Loyalty Day on 19 Calistril, Day of Bones on 3 Pharast, Firstbloom and Planting Week at the vernal equinox, Currentseve on 7 Gozran, Taxfest on 15 Gozran, Archerfeast on 3 Erastus, First Brewing in the first week of Rova, Signing Day on the second Oathday of Rova, Market's Door at the autumn equinox, Even-Tongue Day on 14 Neth, and Winter Week in the third week of Kuthona. Armasse on 16 Arodus and Allbirth on 30 Lamashan are emerging unofficial observances; Signing Day, Loyalty Day, and Even-Tongue Day are being phased out.

## Current Affairs

Saringallow is approaching full cityhood while another war threatens Isger. Trinelli's modernization agenda has collided with conservative fears, farm owners' anger over theft and northern slums, wartime taxation, and candidates already positioning themselves for the next election. The city enjoys good external relations, especially with Swiftrun, but private conflicts increasingly spill into public life.

The Hellbreakers operate openly enough among trusted residents to maintain a concealed headquarters and a candle workshop used as a decoy and funding source. Support ranges from medical neutrality and quiet neighborhood complicity to disguises, equipment, baked goods, weapons, dockside second chances, and coded messages. Captain Jordis opposes their methods, while the Temple of Erastil refuses to deny them aid.

## Key Figures and Factions

### Civic and Religious Leaders

- [[Sandra Trinelli]] - Prudent mayor pursuing modernization, administrative reform, and Saringallow's transition into a fully organized city.
- [[Jordis Serelli]] - Determined captain who runs the city watch with the discipline of the orphanage where they grew up.
- [[Ildris Ruvarra]] - Long-serving head priest of Erastil, focused on food security, farming, and aid without political discrimination.

### Rebels and Community Figures

- [[Elleri Smalltoes|Elleri "Gram" Smalltoes]] - Reclusive elderly candler, escaped Chelaxian slave, Hellbreaker recruit, and architect of the League's candle code.
- [[Maire Phelan]] - Traveling storyteller turned resident who uses hopeful tales to answer Isgeri and Chelaxian neglect.
- [[Old Hawk]] - Elderly fearmonger convinced the Sarinis will return to reclaim the city.
- [[Hellbreakers League]] - Anti-Chelaxian rebels whose covert operations are woven through Saringallow's businesses and neighborhoods.
- [[Stonechewer tribe]] - Goblin and hobgoblin immigrants who built a thriving community after others refused them housing.
- [[Oasis Rain Showers]]: Five outspoken Magaambyan graduates whose performances and teaching helped normalize magic.

## Key Locations

### Historic and Civic Sites

- [[Sarini Manor]] - Half-derelict noble estate associated with Saringallow's founding, the Sarinis' suspected crimes, and the later demonic corruption crisis.
- [[Gallow Farms]] - Six farms that have maintained the city's emergency food reserve since its foundation and now form a political bloc.
- [[Chitterstone Road]] - Unofficial name of the vibrant goblinoid neighborhood created by the Stonechewer tribe, famed for masons, builders, and mercenaries.
- [[Pauper's Roof]] and [[Noble Folly]] - Historic ramparts that saved the city during the Goblinblood Wars; the [[Noble Folly]] gate and its dangerous path remain a traditional dare for children.
- [[City Watch Barracks]] - Fortified complex inside the [[Noble Folly]] gate with towers, yard, stables, mess hall, and quarters for two dozen guards.
- [[Gallow's Graveyard]] - Burial ground as old as the city, now kept by [[Nolaria Wintren]]. Her mistletoe canopy wards against evil, and its cuttings become protective bracelets and wreaths.
- [[Traveler's Square]] - Inns, eateries, and shops serving [[Conerica Highway]] travelers surround a functional, guarded gallows. Morning performers, often [[Maire Phelan]], entertain visitors and wake late sleepers.
- [[City Hall]] - Mansion at triangular [[Crossroad Plaza]] containing the mayor's residence and office, town records, census and deed registry, rural cartography department, and an unfinished courthouse awaiting a judge. City Historian [[Lupia Marietti]] has found politically sensitive records about the region before Isger's founding.

### Faith, Welfare, and Rebellion

- [[Temple of Erastil (Saringallow)|Temple of Erastil]] - Likely the city's oldest surviving structure after [[Sarini Manor]]. Centuries of expansion support healing, food, and shelter, making Erastil the city's patron and limiting Chelaxian faiths' influence.
- [[Hellbreakers' Headquarters]] - Concealed two-story house bought cheaply after the League helped the [[Vazarros]] relocate. It is legally owned by [[Rhokrag]], with [[Phoebe]] and [[Alceo]] as tenants; their regular consecration has earned neighbors' protective silence.
- [[Everbloom Imports Warehouse]] - Fictitious dockside trading concern that conceals the Hellbreakers' principal store of weapons, armor, food, and field supplies.
- [[Saringallow's Sundries]] - Cooperative outfitter with a new pigeon-courier service to major settlements. [[Logas]]-trained scribe [[Abdil Nestthew]] tends the birds and writes letters for customers but strongly dislikes conspiracies.
- [[Saringallow Orchard]] - Apple orchard donated to Erastil's temple after recurring post-crisis infestations and years of consecration. Its successful cooperative sells preserves and dried apples to fund a future school.

### Shops and Hospitality

- [[Witch's End Tavern]] - Conservative tavern run by [[Alicie Kruptin]] under a strict divine-magic-only policy and a powerful suppression effect whose source she refuses to identify.
- [[Dependable Drummady's]] - Cobbler shop turned fashionable haberdashery and boutique. Influential owner [[Drummady Laterna]] covertly supplies Hellbreaker disguises and holds shares in both local cooperatives.
- [[The Lucky Anvil]] - Smithy of [[Ludoff Smeltbristle]], a dwarf caravan tinkerer who fell in love with the city and now secretly crafts for the Hellbreakers.
- [[Gerty's Hearty Breads]] - Bakery now run at the ovens by former apprentice [[Noemi Tauralio]] and [[Xerra Bluebottle]] because war veteran [[Gerty Keronna]]'s injured leg causes chronic pain. [[Xerra Bluebottle|Xerra]] passes leftovers to the Hellbreakers.
- [[Merchant's Square]] - Site of loud daytime and quiet nighttime auctions scheduled by corrupt fur trader [[Petrello Pindlion]], who manipulates sellers for higher commissions and hints at a mayoral run backed by foreign trade ties through his children in [[Druma]] and [[Cheliax]].
- [[Pricknettle's Potions and Poultices]] - Overworked gnome alchemist [[Majara Pricknettle]] hires struggling newcomers as contractors, remembering her own difficult integration after emigrating from [[Umok]].
- [[Queenly Quriosities]] - Early magic shop whose owner, [[Lukus Melchian]], eases customers' fears through his motherly "Mama Yaga" fortune-teller persona and offers his private magical library to sincere readers.
- [[The Fiend's Finale]] - Historic [[Fortnight Inn]], refurbished by [[Morvinarr Albusin]] and known for his cooking and the resident band [[Oasis Rain Showers]], whose five members represent the Magaambya's branches.
- [[Beacon Candles Workshop]] - Hellbreaker-owned warehouse deliberately retained as a decoy. [[Elleri Smalltoes|Gram]]'s carved candles finance the League and encode messages through color, pattern, and shape.
- [[Conerica Docks]] - Increasingly busy docks overseen by meticulous wharfinger [[Bartle Rudderport]], who fears smugglers will damage the city's reputation but quietly teaches the Hellbreakers to improve their subterfuge.
- **Dockside Row** - Warehouse-lined street opposite the southern docks, busy with stevedores and cargo carts throughout the day; Everbloom Imports occupies its partially isolated eastern end.
