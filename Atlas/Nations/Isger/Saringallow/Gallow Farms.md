The Gallow Farms are six farms around [[Saringallow]] charged with maintaining the city's emergency food reserve since its founding. Some remain in the same families after generations; others have changed owners through sale.

## Current Affairs

New slums along the north wall and increasing crop theft have turned the owners against [[Sandra Trinelli]]. Their preferred mayoral candidate is Gerudina Mezeret, the meticulous halfling who is the longest-serving owner.

Gerudina proposes social housing and penalties for unregulated construction, but critics fault her for lacking positions on most other issues. The farms' campaign places food security, urban growth, and class tension at the center of Saringallow's next election.

## Key Figures and Factions

- Gerudina Mezeret: Senior farm owner and prospective mayoral challenger.
- [[Sandra Trinelli]]: Incumbent mayor blamed by the farmers for unchecked settlement and theft.
