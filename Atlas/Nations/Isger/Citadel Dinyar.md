Citadel Dinyar is a sturdy fortress in the [[Aspodell Mountains]] and the headquarters of the [[Order of the Godclaw]].

## Current Affairs

After recent changes in the order's promoted deities and the rising threat of war between [[Cheliax]] and [[Andoran]], Hellknights from Citadel Dinyar have begun traveling into the countryside more often.

Rather than simply demanding obedience, many who worship the Talons of the Godclaw focus on teaching common folk the benefits of ordered, disciplined life. This has caused friction with more traditional Godclaw Hellknights, who have returned to the citadel to prevent new armigers from being drawn too far into the newer philosophy.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Order of the Godclaw]]: Hellknight order headquartered at Citadel Dinyar.
- Talons of the Godclaw: Pantheon promoted by the order's newer religious faction.
