Merrywim is a small riverside town once known mainly for shell jewelry and quiet rural life.

## Current Affairs

After the [[Godsrain]], Merrywim's people became hedonistic nihilists, terrified by the proof that even a god could die and determined to enjoy life as much as possible. The town became known for enormous nightly parties and drinking that lasted until sundown.

The revelry has recently stopped because a massive creature has been spotted in the [[Conerica River]]. Local fishers tried to capture it but returned with only broken nets and lines. Merrywim wants the threat solved so it can return to celebration.
