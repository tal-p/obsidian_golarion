Good Home is a small town abandoned during the [[Goblinblood Wars]] and later claimed by [[Mama Grimclaw]]. The iron hag renamed it and uses it to hide the children she kidnaps.

## Features

Mama Grimclaw shields the town with magic so outsiders do not notice it. The settlement remains in working condition, and the kidnapped children are taught self-sufficiency so they never need to leave.

## Current Affairs

Travelers who wander too close are sometimes murdered so Mama Grimclaw can give their possessions to her children.
