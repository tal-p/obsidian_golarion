Dustpawn is a former iron-mining town near the Druman border. After its mines were exhausted, the town rebuilt its economy around goats, selling milk, butter, cheese, meat, and hides to merchants from [[Isger]], [[Druma]], and [[Andoran]].

## Current Affairs

Heavy trade traffic has made Dustpawn's roads attractive to bandits, many of whom hide in the old mining tunnels. The town once paid the smuggler Elladas Demos to keep attacks down, but she was recently found decapitated in the town square.

Some locals believe [[House Thrune]] killed Elladas after she spread conspiracy theories about Abrogail II's lineage; others think she faked her death and fled to Druma with years of extorted wealth. Since then, merchants have grown wary of the Dustpawn route, and Mayor Ruprecht Haesmith's appeals to [[Elidir]] have gone unanswered.

## Key Figures and Factions

- Elladas Demos: Smuggler whose death or disappearance destabilized Dustpawn's trade.
- Ruprecht Haesmith: Anxious mayor seeking aid from Elidir.
