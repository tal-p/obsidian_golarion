Isger's Fist is the fortress complex of the [[Isgeri Army]] in [[Elidir]]. Its name reflects the idea that the military is the sword wielded by the state.

## Features

The central castle houses command operations, while surrounding grounds include barracks, stables, and other infrastructure needed to support the country's armed forces. From here, the army prioritizes the capital, the Conerica Straits, and the trade routes Cheliax expects Isger to protect.

## Current Affairs

The fortress has become even more important since the [[Battle of Hellknight Hill]], as soldiers have been pulled toward Elidir to defend the capital. This reinforces rural resentment toward [[Hedvend VI]], whose earlier postwar policy already favored the city over the countryside.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Isgeri Army]]: The force headquartered in the complex.
- [[Hedvend VI]]: Steward whose policies determine the army's priorities.
- [[Trade Office]]: Civil partner in keeping Elidir's trade routes moving.
