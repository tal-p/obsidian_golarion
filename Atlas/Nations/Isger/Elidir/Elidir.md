Elidir is the capital of [[Isger]] and the country's central trade hub, built along the [[Conerica River]] where traffic between [[Cheliax]], [[Druma]], [[Molthune]], and [[Andoran]] pours through the city. Its wealth insulates it from much of Isger's suffering, but the city is scarred by foreign domination, class resentment, war losses, refugees, orphans, wounded veterans, and fear that the conflict around [[Breachill]] may finally reach the capital.

## Geography

Elidir's growth has followed the Conerica rather than spreading evenly outward. The city began on one bank, quickly expanded to both sides to increase dock space, and has continued to stretch up and down the river in service to trade.

The riverfront is dominated by docks, warehouses, customs houses, and laborers' neighborhoods. Wealthier residents built manors on the surrounding hills, first for safety from bandits and later to separate themselves from dock noise, crowds, floods, and unrest. Many elite estates still keep private walls even within the city's larger defenses.

Elidir sits in the foothills of the [[Menador Mountains]], where winter storms can bury the city in snow. The Conerica keeps flowing through the cold, so trade continues while laborers face the worst danger. Spring melt can flood the lower city, leaving the poorer neighborhoods muddy while the hilltop estates remain dry.

## History

Long before Taldan rule, the site of Elidir served as a seasonal gathering place for the native Isgeri tribes. In the first millennium AR, local tribes began building roundhouses there for trade, news, and tribal meetings.

Taldor's Seventh Army of Exploration recognized the site's advantages during its campaign against the Isgeri. After the tribes' defeat, General [[Xerlidia Elidos]] made the army's camp permanent and named the settlement after herself. Elidir grew for roughly 2,000 years into a metropolis of about 20,000 people and a major center of trade.

The Even-Tongued Conquest devastated the city. Elidir became a center of the struggle for Isger until [[Cheliax]] seized the province from [[Taldor]], and resources that might have rebuilt the city instead flowed to its new patron. After the climactic Battle of Elidir, many survivors left for Isger's outskirts or for foreign lands. The city returned to trade but never regained its first-millennium glory.

During the [[Goblinblood Wars]], Elidir itself saw little fighting because its elites focused on protecting the capital while the countryside burned. After the war gutted the [[Isgeri Army]], [[Hedvend VI]] ordered the remaining soldiers to defend Elidir and vital routes rather than rural communities. The capital still lives with the resentment of refugees, veterans, and survivors who remember that choice.

## Government & Military

Elidir is governed by [[Hedvend VI]], the Chelaxian-appointed steward of Isger, who rules at the sufferance of Queen Abrogail Thrune II. Cheliax gives him a loose hand in the capital as long as trade continues.

Hedvend relies chiefly on the [[Isgeri Army]] and the [[Trade Office]]. The army patrols the Conerica Straits against bandits and monsters, while the Trade Office manages docks, warehouses, taxes, tariffs, fees, caravanserai, and the city's labor supply.

Elidir's laws heavily favor river commerce. Ships traveling between Druma and Cheliax take priority over local traffic, and many elites exploit the rule by keeping vessels nominally tied to one of those nations. Crimes within 100 yards of the river are punished more harshly than the same crimes elsewhere, and unauthorized boarding or sinking a vessel is treated as a grave offense.

The city still maintains War Laws from the Goblinblood Wars. Dogs must be allowed to roam freely so they can warn of attack and frighten goblins; public singing was banned for fear it could be mistaken for goblin song, encouraging a rebellious local tradition of poetry and storytelling instead. Wasting food remains criminalized, butchers once had to give intestines to the army for bowstrings, killing dogs drew especially severe penalties during famine, and taking a handful of fruit or vegetables from another garden was tolerated to reduce unrest. Enforcement falls far more heavily on the poor than on the elite.

## People & Culture

Elidir's human majority descends from both Taldan conquerors and native Isgeri, whose identities blended over centuries. Most native Elidirans no longer define themselves as purely Taldan or Kellid.

Halflings are the next largest community. Many families were brought through Chelaxian oppression, while others came because Elidir offered better chances to escape servitude than the heart of Cheliax. The Emancipation Edict drew still more halflings despite the city's limited resources.

Goblins have become more common since [[Oprak]]'s founding and broader shifts in attitudes toward goblinoids. Old prohibitions on goblinoids entering Elidir have largely been forgotten, and many goblins are now treated as cheap labor, though tension remains between new arrivals and human survivors of the Goblinblood Wars.

Elidir sees enough trade that elves, dwarves, gnomes, aiuvarin, dromaar, and other peoples are uncommon but unremarkable. The city presents itself as a Chelaxian-style cosmopolitan capital, but the imitation is thin: most residents honor [[Asmodeus]] and Chelaxian holidays such as Signing Day and Even-Tongued Day only perfunctorily.

Class divides shape daily life. Elites flaunt wealth they can still control despite their political dependence on Cheliax, hosting galas, importing food, living in gated manors, and chasing fashions from [[Egorian]] that Chelaxian visitors mock as provincial. Poorer residents answer deprivation with mutual aid, treating small acts of charity as "yanking the devil's tail" - proof that Cheliax has not broken the Isgeri spirit.

Elidiran clothing and homes often seek beauty without luxury. Common residents favor wool, linen, bright colors, delicate embroidery, glass beads, pierced balusters, colorful bead hangings, and painted frieze boards in place of costly jewels and imported materials.

### Faith

[[Asmodeus]] has the largest official presence in Elidir, followed by [[Abadar]]. Most residents are too occupied with survival, trade, or pleasure to be deeply devout, and many attend Asmodean worship more from fear of seeming disloyal to Cheliax than from piety.

[[Nocticula]]'s faithful are split by her ascension. [[Sevizoy]] leads a reformed congregation devoted to the Redeemer Queen from New Dream Designs, while [[Mistress Erity]] controls the old temple and assassin network at [[Sweet Embrace]]. Their holy war remains mostly hidden in dark alleys but threatens to spill into public view.

Agents of [[Urgathoa]] quietly exploit the city's malaise. Led in secret by [[Luminia Bell]], they tempt elites toward gluttony and excess and whisper to the poor that they deserve more, while maneuvering capable clerics of other faiths into opportunities elsewhere so Elidir is served by less effective religious leaders.

## Current Affairs

Elidir is insulated from most external threats by the army's focus on the Conerica Straits. Bandits and undead prey on travelers outside the city walls, but the capital's sharper conflicts come from wealth disparity, Chelaxian rule, religious violence, and the consequences of Hedvend's neglect.

The [[Battle of Hellknight Hill]] has intensified debate over Cheliax's rule and Isgeri rights. Rebellious youths call for Hedvend to step down after the death of a foreign diplomat, and some elites quietly agree because they see an opening to replace him.

Charity events are common but often serve more as elite social occasions than meaningful relief. The lower classes, veterans, and refugees remain a living reminder of the city government's failures during and after the Goblinblood Wars.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Hedvend VI]] - Chelaxian-appointed steward whose focus on trade and the capital has made him widely hated.
- [[Trade Office]] - Bureaucratic arm that controls docks, taxes, tariffs, warehouses, caravanserai, and labor.
- [[Isgeri Army]] - Military force concentrated around Elidir and the [[Conerica Straits]].
- [[Adasely Tisandera]] - Ambitious caravanserai operator seeking to dominate that market.
- [[Cygnetus Frawn]] - Fashion leader and subtle Chelaxian loyalist among Elidir's elite.
- [[Dwingini Berylbelt]] - Archbanker of Abadar who privately believes Elidir's dominance strangles Isger's wider prosperity.
- [[Luminia Bell]] - Popular chef and secret leader of Urgathoa's followers in Isger.
- [[Queen of Conerica]] - Mysterious pirate captain raiding merchant ships on the Conerica.
- [[Sevizoy]] - Former assassin and leader of Nocticula's reformed faithful in Elidir.
- [[Mistress Erity]] - Assassin who controls [[Sweet Embrace]] and opposes Nocticula's Redeemer Queen faithful.

## Key Locations

- [[Chelaxian Quarter]] - Walled district also called the Devils' Court, centered on the Thrune embassy and wealthy Chelaxian expatriates.
- [[Conerica Landing]] - Heavily guarded docks and warehouses that form the heart of Elidir's trade.
- [[Trade House]] - Sprawling tax and customs office near the docks, notorious for corruption.
- [[Isger's Fist]] - Fortress complex of the Isgeri military, including command operations, barracks, stables, and other army infrastructure.
- [[Kingsthrone Cathedral]] - Five-towered Asmodean cathedral built at Thrune command and attended largely from fear.
- [[Xerlidia Park]] - Ancient pact-protected park containing the [[Isgeri Circle]] and [[Memorial to Lost Soldiers]].
- [[Sweet Embrace]] - Old temple of Nocticula and assassin guild now controlled by [[Mistress Erity]].
- [[New Dream Designs]] - Tattoo shop and secret shrine of [[Sevizoy]]'s Redeemer Queen congregation.
- [[Gyve and Moil]] - City prison and attached labor camp for debtors, political prisoners, criminals, and minor offenders.
- [[Coffer Hall]] - Low-key temple of Abadar, weakened by Urgathoan maneuvering against its most capable clergy.
- [[Druma Road Gate]] and [[Grand Cheliax Gate]] - The city's major eastern and western trade gates, the latter guarded beneath carved nessari devil statues.
- [[Hedvend Park]] - Steward-named park renovated by [[Hedvend VI]] with a fountain of [[Abrogail Thrune II]] and imported animals.
- [[Iron Strop]] - Former Gorumite veterans' home whose caretakers and patients became nihilistic after Gorum's death.
- [[Home to Kazhlik]] - Two-thousand-year-old dwarven tavern known for Five Kings Mountains fare.
- [[Myrogy's Menagerie]] - Fading creature showcase whose owner seeks dangerous new attractions.
- [[Night Market]] - Market outside the [[Druma Road Gate]] where daytime trade gives way to stolen cargo after dark.
- [[Rampways]] - Roads around Elidir used by traders and the army, but vulnerable to bandits from the hills.
- [[Spellbinder's Stage]] - Minimalist theater whose subversive owner may stage the infamous play _Abrogail I_.
- [[Succubus's Sash]] - Boutique selling devil-themed fashion and late-arriving Egorian trends.
- [[White Stag Inn]] - Busy riverside inn where dock workers, merchants, and stevedores seek work and workers.
