Kingsthrone Cathedral is the center of [[Asmodeus]]' faith in [[Elidir]], built with taxpayer money at direct [[House Thrune]] command. Its five ruby-studded towers dominate the skyline as a visible reminder of Chelaxian power.

## Features

Merchants, dottari, and congregants constantly gather around the cathedral's grand doors. Worship is strictly scheduled, and the cathedral is the city's most popular place of worship less because of devotion than because residents fear appearing disloyal to [[Cheliax]].

## Current Affairs

Despite Asmodeus' public dominance, Elidir's religious life is unstable. [[Urgathoa]]'s followers work unseen among the elite and the poor, while [[Nocticula]]'s divided faithful fight in secret. The cathedral's power keeps rival faiths cautious, but it has not rooted them out.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[House Thrune]]: Ordered the cathedral's construction.
- [[Luminia Bell]]: Secret Urgathoan leader exploiting the city's spiritual weaknesses.
- [[Sevizoy]] and [[Mistress Erity]]: Rival Nocticulan leaders whose conflict remains outside the cathedral's control.
