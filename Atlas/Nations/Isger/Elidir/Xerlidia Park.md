Xerlidia Park is an ancient public park in [[Elidir]] named for the city's founder, [[Xerlidia Elidos]]. It survives because of an ironclad pact she made with the early Isgeri tribes and remains one of the few spiritual places in Isger untouched by Cheliax's fiendish presence.

## History

The park preserves older Isgeri religious memory inside the capital. At its heart stands the Isgeri Circle, a 2,500-year-old ring of standing stones around a badly worn altar to [[Urgathoa]] in an ancient Kellid incarnation as a harvest goddess. The circle was originally raised to appease the early Isgeri, and every attempt to remove it has failed; superstition says those who try grow ill and die.

## Features

Animists tend the park and channel spirits of the ancient Kellid Speakers of the Pale faith. They welcome visitors to walk the paths, commune with nature, and seek contact with the dead.

The park also holds the Memorial to Lost Soldiers, a gleaming steel sculpture made from broken swords and armor. Many Elidirans visit the park not out of formal faith but out of hope, drawn to a place that does not feel tainted by Hell.

## Current Affairs

Xerlidia Park quietly contrasts with the state-backed prominence of [[Kingsthrone Cathedral]]. Its continued survival gives Elidirans a rare civic symbol not defined by [[Cheliax]], [[Asmodeus]], or the capital's trade machine.
