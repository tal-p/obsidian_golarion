The Chelaxian Quarter, called the Devils' Court by locals, is a walled district of [[Elidir]] surrounding the official [[House Thrune]] embassy. It is home to Chelaxian expatriates, wealthy loyalists, and the most luxurious houses in the capital.

## Features

The district's residents are known for excess, imported fashions, and cultivated displays of loyalty to [[Cheliax]]. Dottari and devils guard the quarter, enforcing the law brutally against trespassers and anyone who threatens its residents.

## Current Affairs

The quarter embodies the special protection Chelaxians receive in Isger's capital. Its security and wealth sharpen resentment among Elidirans who live closer to the riverfront, especially as criticism of [[Hedvend VI]] and Chelaxian rule grows after the [[Battle of Hellknight Hill]].

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[House Thrune]]: The embassy anchors Chelaxian influence in Elidir.
- [[Cygnetus Frawn]]: A prominent social figure whose parties and fashion movements quietly push support for Cheliax.
