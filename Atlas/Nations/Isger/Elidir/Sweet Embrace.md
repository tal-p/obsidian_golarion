Sweet Embrace is an unassuming wooden tower in [[Elidir]] that long housed both a low-end brothel and the city's secret temple of [[Nocticula]]. It also sheltered an assassin guild operating in Nocticula's name.

## History

After Nocticula's ascension as the Redeemer Queen, the Elidir temple split. [[Mistress Erity]] won control of Sweet Embrace and continued both its public and hidden trades while rejecting the goddess's redeemed identity.

## Current Affairs

Mistress Erity and her assassins now fight to eliminate the remnant of the cult that accepted Nocticula's change. They have found a new divine patron for their prayers, though it is unclear whether even they know who grants their power.

Their chief opposition is [[Sevizoy]]'s congregation at New Dream Designs, where Nocticula's Redeemer Queen faithful organize, create magical tattoos to raise funds, and seek mercenaries for the conflict.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Mistress Erity]]: Assassin leader who controls Sweet Embrace.
- [[Sevizoy]]: Leader of the rival Redeemer Queen congregation.
- [[Nocticula]]: Goddess whose transformation caused the schism.
