Conerica Landing is the network of wooden docks and warehouses along both banks of the [[Conerica River]] in [[Elidir]]. It is the city's chief industrial and commercial zone, and one of the most heavily guarded parts of the capital.

## Features

The Landing handles river traffic, cargo storage, customs oversight, labor hiring, and the constant movement of merchants between [[Cheliax]], [[Druma]], [[Molthune]], and [[Andoran]]. Its importance shapes the city's laws: crimes near the river are punished more harshly, foreign trade vessels receive priority, and interference with boats is treated as a serious offense.

The nearby Trade House oversees taxes, tariffs, fees, warehouses, caravanserai, and trade regulation. Its broad authority creates many opportunities for corruption, which is usually ignored unless it becomes too visible.

## Current Affairs

The [[Isgeri Army]] patrols the Conerica Straits to keep trade moving, but the [[Queen of Conerica]] has become a scourge of merchant vessels. Her pirates strike quickly, raid only trade ships, and vanish with cargo before authorities can respond.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Trade Office]]: The bureaucracy that oversees Elidir's trade apparatus from the Trade House.
- [[Queen of Conerica]]: Mysterious pirate leader targeting merchant ships.
- [[Isgeri Army]]: Military force charged with protecting the river trade.
