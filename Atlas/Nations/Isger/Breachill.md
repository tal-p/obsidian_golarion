Breachill is a young town in the shadow of the [[Five Kings Mountains]]. Its early history is mysterious: in 4520 AR, fifty humans with no memories of their identities or arrival were found there by the wizard Lamond Breachton, who helped them turn their camp into a town.

## History

Breachton remained as a paternal guide for years, and the grateful townsfolk named the settlement after him. After his disappearance, they raised a statue in his honor and continued his benevolent civic legacy.

In 4638 AR, the newly formed [[Order of the Nail]] built [[Citadel Altaerein]] ten miles northeast of town, though the order later moved to [[Korvosa]].

## Government & Military

Breachill is governed by a town council. During the recent [[Order of the Rack]] occupation, the Hellknights placed most citizens under house arrest, imposed curfews and strict laws, and carried out public executions while searching for [[Hellbreakers League]] members and hidden warshards. The crackdown angered the council, especially Councilor Greta Gardania, who secretly appealed to neighboring countries for aid.

## Current Affairs

Rumors that Breachill harbored the Hellbreakers League and warshards drew the Order of the Rack into town in Gozran 4725 AR. The Hellknights failed to find League members despite several public executions. When Andoran sent retired Eagle Knight Reginald Cormoth to seek a diplomatic solution in Sarenith 4725 AR, the Hellknights executed him as an alleged spy. Public outrage in Andoran led to an emergency People's Council in [[Almas]], after which Eagle Knights of the [[Steel Falcons]] traveled to Isger.

In Rova 4725 AR, Andoren forces retook Breachill and [[Citadel Altaerein]] during the [[Battle of Hellknight Hill]]. Breachill now faces the aftermath of Andoran's victory, the Hellknights' defeat, and the Hellbreakers' negotiations with Andoran.

## Key Figures and Factions

- Greta Gardania: Breachill councilor who opposed the Hellknight occupation and sought foreign help.
- Lamond Breachton: Wizard credited with helping the original amnesiac settlers found Breachill.
- Reginald Cormoth: Retired Eagle Knight envoy executed by the occupying Hellknights.
- [[Hellbreakers League]]: Rebel group rumored to have hidden members in town.
- [[Order of the Rack]]: Hellknight order that occupied Breachill.

## Key Locations

- [[Citadel Altaerein]]: Former Hellknight citadel ten miles northeast of Breachill.
