Saringallow is an Isgeri city with deep anti-noble and anti-Chelaxian resentment. Its history with [[House Sarini]] makes the city especially hostile to outsiders who appear ready to exploit the populace.

## History

The city began centuries ago as Sarini's Hollow, a garden estate founded by the minor Chelaxian noble family House Sarini. The village that grew around it seemed idyllic, but the Sarinis practiced diabolism long before [[House Thrune]] rose to power. Their rituals killed several of the village's young people.

The citizens rebelled and hanged every Sarini they could find. The settlement became known as Sarini Gallows, eventually shortened into Saringallow.

## People & Culture

Saringallow's people remember the Sarini estate as a warning to predatory nobles. Noble outsiders are made unwelcome, especially anyone bearing the Sarini name.

## Current Affairs

The city's rebellious history likely explains why [[Hellbreakers League]] activity is so common here. Many believe Saringallow may be the organization's headquarters.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Hellbreakers League]]: Rebel organization with strong activity in Saringallow.
- [[House Sarini]]: Chelaxian noble family whose abuses shaped the city's identity.

## Key Locations

- Sarini estate: Old estate that still stands as a warning to Chelaxian nobles.
