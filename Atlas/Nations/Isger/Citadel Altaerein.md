Citadel Altaerein is a Hellknight fortress a short distance from [[Breachill]]. It was built as the original base of the [[Order of the Nail]] and has repeatedly changed hands during Isger's recent crises.

## History

The [[Order of the Nail]] was founded in 4638 AR and built the citadel near Breachill that same year before moving to [[Korvosa]] in 4682 AR. Fewer than three dozen Hellknights remained for decades, and the fortress was fully abandoned in 4711 AR.

## Current Affairs

After the [[Godsrain]], Isgeri officials suspected warshards might be hidden in Breachill or Citadel Altaerein. In Arodus 4725 AR, Chelaxian supplies allowed [[Order of the Rack]] Hellknights to fortify the citadel, sanctify its buildings with infernal wards, and conjure a diabolical dragon. The citadel was besieged during the [[Battle of Hellknight Hill]] in Rova 4725 AR, and the Hellknights were routed. Its future ownership is uncertain.
