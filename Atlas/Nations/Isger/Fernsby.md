Fernsby is a conservative farming village north of the [[Chitterwood]], surrounded by farms and orchards. It supplies nearby [[Stumpfield]] with food and practical goods, making their uneasy relationship important to both communities.

## Features

Thatched-roof cottages, many with flower-filled porches, line Fernsby's streets. A large oak shades the central village green, whose carefully maintained flowerbeds and shrubs are a point of civic pride.

## People & Culture

Fernsby's residents are polite but guarded with strangers. Their practical, conservative outlook contrasts with Stumpfield's unconventional community of goblin and human artists.

## Current Affairs

Repeated nighttime vandalism of the village green was initially blamed on Stumpfield's goblins, threatening to end trade between the settlements. The vandals left winding tracks rather than footprints: they were goblin-headed snakes directed by [[Hissbite]], who hoped to poison relations between goblins and humans. Evidence from their lair in a mossy ravine outside the village cleared Stumpfield of responsibility.

## Key Locations

- **Village green** — Fernsby's cherished communal space, centered on a mature oak and decorated with flowers.
- **Mossy ravine** — A forty-foot-deep fissure outside the village where Hissbite and several other goblin snakes made their lair beneath a sheltered overhang.
