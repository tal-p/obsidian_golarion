Haugin's Ear is a quiet fishing village on the [[River Keld]]. Its residents trade fresh and cured fish with passing vessels, especially Chelaxian military and trade ships, and with miners panning for gold in the [[Aspodell Mountains]] foothills.

## Features

The village is named for a 30-foot-tall rock outcropping near its docks that resembles the half-buried head and ear of a giant lying on his side. Local legend claims it is part of the petrified remains of Haugin, a flesh-eating giant cursed by a Taldan wizard.

Some families sell tamed firefoot fennecs, which are considered symbols of good luck in the region.

## Current Affairs

Treasure hunters sometimes visit seeking shattered pieces of Haugin, rumored to hold great power. In the past, locals believed the stone head lured children inside; the true culprit was Marjulla, a sweet hag who devoured them. A visiting treasure hunter recently discovered and slew her, but Marjulla warned that her two sisters would seek retribution.
