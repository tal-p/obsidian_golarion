Elidir is Isger's capital and a major trade hub where the Summerun River meets the North Conerica River. Trade from [[Molthune]], [[Druma]], and even distant [[Cheliax]] flows through the city.

## History

The city stands near the old meeting place of the eleven Isgeri tribes. During the [[Goblinblood Wars]], Elidir escaped much of the destruction that ravaged the countryside because it lay more than 100 miles from the [[Chitterwood]].

That safety preserved the government but damaged its reputation. Many soldiers and rural Isgeri believe Elidir's officials failed to end the war, delayed action, or even profited while common folk suffered.

## Government & Military

Elidir is the seat of [[Hedvend VI]], Isger's Chelaxian-appointed steward. Chelaxian nobles increasingly doubt his competence despite his long loyalty, and rumors of his replacement have circulated for years.

Since the [[Battle of Hellknight Hill]], many soldiers of the [[Isgeri Army]] have been pulled back to protect the capital.

## People & Culture

Elidir is far wealthier than most of Isger. Its elites live lavishly, a stark contrast to the rural communities still recovering from war. Countryside Isgeri often sneer at the capital and view its residents as insulated fools.

## Current Affairs

Hedvend knows Cheliax may replace him and is being pushed by close allies to act more boldly, especially after the defeat of Hellknights at [[Citadel Altaerein]].

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Hedvend VI]]: Steward of Isger and central figure in Elidir's government.
- [[Isgeri Army]]: Military force increasingly concentrated around the capital.
