Swiftrun is a small Erastilian village known for priests who teach homesteading and community defense.

## People & Culture

The community is nearly entirely devoted to [[Erastil]] and places strong emphasis on mutual aid. Instead of individual gardens, Swiftrun maintains a large communal garden tended in turns by everyone.

Tools, carts, and other necessities are shared, and hospitality is treated as the village's greatest virtue.
