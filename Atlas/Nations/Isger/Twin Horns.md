Twin Horns is a small nunnery of the [[Sisterhood of the Golden Erinys]] in the [[Aspodell Mountains]]. It trains aspiring young women as Asmodean sisters.

## Features

Training at the convent is rigorous. The nuns practice religious duties and martial arts from dawn to dusk, and many girls raised in the sisterhood's orphanages eventually travel here to become nuns.

## Current Affairs

The nunnery recently discovered a piece of a [[Warshard]] that affected its livestock, turning its goats into dawnsilver.
