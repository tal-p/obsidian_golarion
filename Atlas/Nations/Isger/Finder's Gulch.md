Finder's Gulch is a haunted former quarry town ruled by [[Illcayna Alonnor]]. Once remote and ordinary, it was overrun by malicious undead several decades ago.

## History

In 4601 AR, Illcayna fed herself to a group of wights and was resurrected as a daughter of [[Urgathoa]]. She slaughtered the villagers of Finder's Gulch, transformed them into undead thralls, and made the ruins her base of operations. From there she has raided Isgeri villages, [[Cheliax]], and [[Molthune]] to expand her undead armies.

## Current Affairs

Illcayna ultimately wants to turn all of Isger toward the worship of [[Urgathoa]], likely by force. Finder's Gulch remains the center of that campaign.
