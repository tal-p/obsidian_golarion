Wolfpoint is a reserve garrison of the [[Isgeri Army]].

## Current Affairs

Since the [[Battle of Hellknight Hill]], Wolfpoint has been left empty because its soldiers were called to active duty elsewhere in Isger. Local river bandits have grown bolder and often use the deserted garrison as a meeting place.
