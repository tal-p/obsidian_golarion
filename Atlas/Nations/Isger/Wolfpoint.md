Wolfpoint is an [[Isgeri Army]] reserve garrison south of the [[Conerica River]], built to house roughly 300 soldiers and officers charged with protecting the river and nearby roads from bandits. Its soldiers were reassigned after the [[Battle of Hellknight Hill]], leaving the strategically placed fort abandoned.

## History

Wolfpoint predates the [[Goblinblood Wars]]. Its wooden palisade burned during the conflict but was later rebuilt.

## Features

The garrison consists of numerous barracks enclosed by a repaired wooden palisade. The surrounding countryside includes abandoned farms and other secluded sites favored by the [[Steel Circle]], an organized bandit gang active across southern [[Isger]].

## Current Affairs

Wolfpoint's soldiers were redeployed elsewhere in [[Isger]] amid tensions along the Andoren border. Their absence emboldened local highwaymen and overwhelmed the local sheriff's ability to contain crime.

The [[Steel Circle]] briefly tried to establish a permanent base inside the garrison, but nightmares drove them to an abandoned farm a mile southeast. The true occupant was a drude drawn to the site's history of calamity, accompanied by pugwampi and mitflit gremlins. After the drude was driven out, [[Runa Bloodhammer]] and the Steel Circle moved into Wolfpoint, promising to prey only upon travelers clearly aligned with [[Cheliax]].

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Isgeri Army]] — Built and staffed Wolfpoint before withdrawing its garrison.
- [[Steel Circle]] — A mobile southern Isgeri bandit gang that claimed the abandoned fort after its haunting ended.
- [[Runa Bloodhammer]] — Wary dwarven leader of the Steel Circle.
