The Lucent Mission is a secluded monastery of the [[Sisterhood of the Golden Erinys]] in the northwestern foothills of the [[Aspodell Mountains]]. It trains novitiates assigned throughout [[Isger]] and safeguards an archive of Asmodean theology, infernal lore, and martial instruction.

## History

Different orders of the sisterhood have used the mission for decades. [[Viro Ahala]] gained access by posing as a Hellknight of the [[Order of the Rack]] and winning [[Sister Endreah]]'s trust. With her aid, he found an archived ritual intended to summon a powerful devil and bind it without the risks of an infernal bargain. He took the ritual text east toward the [[Chitterwood]], intending to turn its power against Isger's goblins and the [[Hellbreakers League]].

## Features

A hidden trail climbs from a trailhead half a day's travel from [[Vordia]] to the wooden monastery. A low stone wall encloses a garden of autumn flowers and bitter grapes tended by novitiates. Within are a simple temple, identical sleeping cells, a dining room decorated with cavorting devils, a kitchen, and a training hall where sisters practice the martial art called Hell's Claw.

The archive contains Asmodean scripture, studies of Hell's hierarchy, illustrated martial primers, and decades of letters. A velavi serves as its infernal archivist. Sisters sometimes borrow its tomes to summon minor devils for menial labor.

## Current Affairs

Endreah and the sisters from [[Bright Embers Orphanage]] occupied the mission while aiding Viro. [[Sister Sylve]] discovered that he had never completed a Hellknight initiation and left to seek help when formal channels failed. After Viro departed, Endreah searched the archive for information that might help [[Cheliax]] retain control of Isger. Captured sisters were returned to Vordia, where Sylve's testimony persuaded the previously sympathetic townspeople to hold them while their actions were investigated.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Sisterhood of the Golden Erinys]] — Uses the mission to train novices and preserve infernal scholarship.
- [[Sister Endreah]] — Superior who granted Viro Ahala access to the archive and its binding ritual.
- [[Sister Sylve]] — Novitiate who exposed Viro as an impostor and revealed the mission's location.
- [[Viro Ahala]] — False Hellknight who exploited the mission's archive to prepare his revenge against the Hellbreakers League.
