Gillamoor was a settlement on the edge of the [[Chitterwood]]. It survived goblin attacks and several forest fires, only to fall to undeath.

## History

Because Gillamoor had a tragic past and a large graveyard, [[Illcayna Alonnor]] chose it as a target in her campaign to infest Isger with undead. In 4702 AR, she cursed a traveling merchant who unknowingly carried the curse to Gillamoor when he died and was buried there. She also placed a curse on the settlement so anyone who died there would rise as one of her ravenous undead thralls.

The curse spread quickly after the merchant's burial. Within weeks, Gillamoor's population had died, fled, or been transformed into undead.

## Current Affairs

The [[Isgeri Army]] initially quarantined the area, believing the disaster to be plague, but abandoned the post after no disease could be found.
