Logas is a riverside city and Isger's former capital. Its buildings and people still bear the scars of the [[Goblinblood Wars]].

## History

Logas was repeatedly under siege during the war, creating a lasting distrust of goblinoids. Until recently, the city regularly fired trebuchets into the nearby [[Chitterwood]] and displayed goblin skulls along its walls.

## Government & Military

Mayor Valenia Remingward, herself a veteran of the war, ended the trebuchet fire and removed the skull displays after taking office in 4723 AR. Some nobles objected, claiming that such policies would look weak to goblin tribes and encourage renewed conflict.

## Current Affairs

Those complaints have largely quieted as Hellknights gather in Logas, either fleeing defeat at the [[Battle of Hellknight Hill]] or arriving from [[Cheliax]] as reinforcements. Tensions are high, though most citizens support Chelaxian rule over Isger.

## Key Figures and Factions

- Valenia Remingward: Progressive mayor and Goblinblood Wars veteran.
- [[Hellknights]]: Present in growing numbers after the Battle of Hellknight Hill.

## Key Locations

- [[Chitterwood]]: Forest near Logas and focus of the city's wartime hostility.
