Bright Embers Orphanage is an Asmodean institution on the eastern outskirts of [[Vordia]], established by the [[Sisterhood of the Golden Erinys]] after the [[Goblinblood Wars]]. Nearly two decades of coercive discipline left a psychic imprint that erupted into a haunting when the sisters abandoned the building.

## History

The orphanage was established in 4701 AR to house children orphaned by the Goblinblood Wars. The sisters prepared them for life under Asmodean law through pro-[[Cheliax]] instruction, repeatedly blaming goblin chaos for their parents' deaths while ignoring the rigid discipline of many hobgoblin commanders.

The children's repressed fear, grief, and frustration accumulated within the structure. Former residents reported slamming doors, cries from the walls, and the sensation of being watched, but the sisters dismissed these incidents and suppressed the manifestations through force of will. After [[Viro Ahala]] persuaded [[Sister Endreah]] to assist him, the nuns transferred their final two wards elsewhere and departed for the [[Lucent Mission]]. Without them, the orphanage's psychic resonance formed unstable spirits and haunts.

## Features

A walled yard surrounds the slightly crooked two-story building. Its leafless trees, dying grass, chipped seesaw, and deteriorating interior aged supernaturally under the haunting's influence. Curtains covered the windows and doors hung crooked while phantom footsteps echoed through the halls.

The ground floor held a dining room and a playroom decorated with murals portraying devils as friendly companions to children. Communal bedrooms occupied the upper floor, while the nuns slept in sparse cells furnished with cots and low padded prayer beams beneath painted pentagrams. Manifestations reflected the children's tightly controlled meals, play, and bedtimes; an attic whisperer formed from their abandonment and despair sustained the haunting.

## Current Affairs

The disappearance of the sisters and the supposed placement of the last two orphans drew local suspicion after teenagers heard noises inside the apparently empty building. Destroying the attic whisperer permanently ended the manifestations. A draft letter found in the nuns' quarters revealed that the sisters had gone to the Lucent Mission for a task intended to glorify [[Asmodeus]]. [[Sister Sylve]] hoped to return and reopen Bright Embers once it was cleansed.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Sisterhood of the Golden Erinys]] — Founded and operated the orphanage as both a relief institution and a center of Asmodean indoctrination.
- [[Sister Endreah]] — Led the resident sisters away to assist Viro Ahala.
- [[Sister Sylve]] — Intended to reopen the orphanage after breaking with Endreah and exposing Viro's deception.
