Vordia is a traditional town of several hundred people northwest of the [[Aspodell Mountains]]. [[Cheliax]] and the [[Sisterhood of the Golden Erinys]] helped rebuild it after the devastation of the [[Goblinblood Wars]], creating an enduring local loyalty to Chelaxian rule and the [[Church of Asmodeus]].

## History

Vordia suffered extensive damage and heavy loss of life during the [[Goblinblood Wars]]. Chelaxian relief efforts included building [[Bright Embers Orphanage]], where the Sisterhood of the Golden Erinys cared for war orphans while instructing them in Asmodean order and pro-Cheliax doctrine.

## People & Culture

Because the sisters cared for Vordia after the war, many residents remain grateful to [[Cheliax]]. Chelaxian national flags and symbols of [[Asmodeus]] fly from nearly every building, forming the most distinctive feature of an otherwise typical, prosperous Isgeri town.

## Current Affairs

The town grew concerned after a mysterious Hellknight visited [[Bright Embers Orphanage]] and its nuns largely vanished from public view. Records claimed that the orphanage's last two children had been placed with families in neighboring towns, but the receiving families' names were illegible. The sisters had instead transferred the children elsewhere and departed for the [[Lucent Mission]], leaving decades of suppressed psychic distress to manifest as a haunting.

## Key Figures and Factions

- [[Sisterhood of the Golden Erinys]] — Long respected for rebuilding the town and operating its orphanage.
- [[Sister Sylve]] — A novitiate who uncovered [[Viro Ahala]]'s deception and sought to prevent his devil-summoning ritual.
- [[Sister Endreah]] — Superior who led Vordia's sisters to aid Viro Ahala.

## Key Locations

- [[Bright Embers Orphanage]] — An Asmodean orphanage founded after the Goblinblood Wars and later overtaken by manifestations of its residents' accumulated distress.
