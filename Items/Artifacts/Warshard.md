Warshards are fragments of the dead god [[Gorum]] scattered across Golarion by the [[Godsrain]] in Neth 4724 AR. Their appearance began a race among factions and powerful individuals to claim them.

## Ownership & Significance

The [[Hellbreakers League]] found a warshard and used it as a revolutionary symbol against [[Cheliax]]. [[Illcayna Alonnor]] and [[Lady Kaltessa Iyis]] also collected warshards for their own purposes, while a fragment at [[Twin Horns]] transformed the nunnery's goats into dawnsilver.

Isgeri officials suspected the Hellbreakers were hiding warshards in [[Breachill]] or [[Citadel Altaerein]], prompting the [[Order of the Rack]] occupation. After the [[Battle of Hellknight Hill]], Andoran promised aid to the Hellbreakers in exchange for a powerful warshard taken back to [[Andoran]].
