*Serving Your Hunger* is the holy text of [[Urgathoa]], combining the practical form of a cookbook with arguments intended to draw readers toward undeath.

## Description

Its recipes describe forbidden meals, while its riddles provoke readers to question mortal limits and accept undeath.

## Ownership & Significance

The book unites the Pallid Princess's gluttony with her rejection of death, presenting each as part of the same religious appetite.
