Mother's Maw is [[Urgathoa]]'s divine representative, a giant winged skull surrounded by flies and adoring undead. It carries out the Pallid Princess's commands regardless of how destructive or bizarre they might be.

## Background & Role

As Urgathoa's herald, Mother's Maw bears the goddess's will directly into the world. Its leathery bat wings carry its immense skull through the air, while undead thralls gather in its wake.

## Current Affairs

Mother's Maw transports allies, creates new undead, and devours those placed in its path. It accepts every divine command without hesitation.

## Ambitions & Influence

When not serving Urgathoa, Mother's Maw seeks unfamiliar sensual experiences. It eagerly discusses food, wine, perfume, and other pleasures, reflecting the appetites at the heart of its goddess's faith.

## Connections

- [[Urgathoa]] is the goddess whose will it enacts.
- Undead creatures serve as both its entourage and beneficiaries of its power.
