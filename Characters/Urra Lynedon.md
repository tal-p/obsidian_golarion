Urra Lynedon is a vampire count who cultivates diseases as though they were rare flowers. [[Urgathoa]] gave this work purpose to an immortal existence that Urra had found tedious.

## Background & Role

Immortality had become boring to Urra before Urgathoa directed her toward the study and cultivation of disease. She has since made infection both her vocation and her principal form of devotion.

## Current Affairs

Urra uses the bodies of devoted mortal consorts to nurture strains of bacteria, fungi, and viruses gathered from across [[Golarion]]. Her household functions as both court and living garden for these afflictions.

## Ambitions & Influence

She distributes the results to other Urgathoans seeking unfamiliar sensations or the fever-driven visions associated with their goddess. Her collection gives the wider faith access to diseases from distant lands.

## Connections

- [[Urgathoa]] gave direction to her immortal life.
- Her mortal consorts sustain the diseases she cultivates and shares.
