Many-Limbs Kimmato is an undead halfling herexen who turned from [[Sarenrae]] after their spouse died in childbirth. Their grief, surgical blasphemy, and attacks on their former faith earned the favor of [[Urgathoa]].

## Background & Role

Kimmato was once a physician devoted to Sarenrae. Desperate to remain united with their spouse, they grafted the still-warm remains onto their own body, rejecting the faith and principles they had previously served. Urgathoa answered the defiance by granting Kimmato undeath and a new title.

## Current Affairs

Kimmato now attacks Sarenrae's temples and worshippers in Urgathoa's name. They graft parts of new victims onto their amalgamated body, which moves through the coordination of hundreds of limbs.

## Ambitions & Influence

Their campaign turns private loss into vengeance against an entire religion. Each victim becomes both a desecration of Sarenrae's ideals and a visible testament to Urgathoa's favor.

## Connections

- [[Sarenrae]] was their former patron and is now the focus of their vengeance.
- [[Urgathoa]] rewarded their blasphemy with undeath.
