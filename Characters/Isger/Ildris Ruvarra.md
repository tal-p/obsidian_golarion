Ildris Ruvarra is the kind head priest of [[Erastil]]'s temple in [[Saringallow]] and a local pillar of faith. After nearly twenty years in the role, he focuses on the city's health, food security, and agricultural needs rather than public alignment in Isger's worsening political conflict.

## Background & Role

Ildris leads the temple whose healing, nutrition, and shelter work made Erastil Saringallow's widely recognized patron and prevented Chelaxian faiths from gaining much influence.

## Current Affairs

He refuses to associate publicly with any political side, but neither he nor his staff has denied aid to anyone, including visitors who openly identify themselves as members of the [[Hellbreakers League]]. Temple cooperatives at Saringallow Orchard sell apple preserves and dried fruit to fund a future school.

## Ambitions & Influence

Ildris uses the temple's resources to stabilize Saringallow rather than to advance a faction. His practical neutrality makes the faith a rare institution trusted across the city's divisions.

## Connections

- Nolaria Wintren, his adventurous adopted daughter, became groundskeeper of Gallow's Graveyard, possibly as an act of adult rebellion against him.
- [[Hellbreakers League]] receives care from his temple without endorsement or refusal.
