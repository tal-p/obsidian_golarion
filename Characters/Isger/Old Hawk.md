Old Hawk is one of [[Saringallow]]'s oldest residents and an unrelenting keeper of its fears. He is convinced [[House Sarini]] will soon return to reclaim the city and prepares himself and anyone who will listen for that day.

## Background & Role

Hawk embraces numerous superstitions, lines his home with salt, and consumes enormous quantities of garlic. His warnings preserve the old Saringallow habit of turning wariness of power into ritual protection.

## Current Affairs

Younger residents humor him by accepting garlic garlands and promising to train for a fight. Older residents have grown tired of his constant alarmism.

## Ambitions & Influence

Hawk wants Saringallow ready for the Sarinis' imagined descent. Although few believe him, his persistence keeps the city's violent founding and fear of aristocracy present in daily life.

## Connections

- [[Sarini Manor]] is the abandoned estate at the heart of his fears.
- [[Saringallow]] both indulges and resents his warnings.
