Mistress Erity is the assassin who controls [[Sweet Embrace]], the old secret temple of [[Nocticula]] in [[Elidir]]. She rejects Nocticula's redeemed identity and leads the faction hunting the goddess's reformed faithful.

## Background & Role

After Nocticula's ascension as the Redeemer Queen split the city's faithful, Erity seized control of Sweet Embrace. The tower continues to operate as both brothel and assassin stronghold.

## Current Affairs

Erity and her assassins seek to eliminate the survivors who embraced Nocticula's transformation. Their prayers now receive power from another patron, though it is unclear whether even they know who answers them.

## Connections

- [[Sevizoy]] leads the rival Redeemer Queen congregation at New Dream Designs.
- [[Sweet Embrace]] is her base of power.
- [[Nocticula]]'s transformation caused the schism she refuses to accept.
