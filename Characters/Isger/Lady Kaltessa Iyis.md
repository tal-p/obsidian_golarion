Lady Kaltessa Iyis, also called the Widow Queen, is a seemingly immortal priestess of [[Mammon]] who dwells in the [[Iron Rose]] in the [[Menador Mountains]].

## Background & Role

Kaltessa's unnaturally long life and devilish servants come from an infernal pact with Mammon. She watches [[Isger]] from her fortress and plots on her master's behalf.

## Current Affairs

Her devotion to Mammon rather than [[Asmodeus]] makes her a rogue element within Chelaxian territory. After acquiring a [[Warshard]] following the [[Godsrain]] and watching tensions rise between [[Cheliax]] and [[Andoran]], she appears ready to act.

## Ambitions & Influence

Kaltessa designs to rule Isger and perhaps one day Cheliax. She schemes from hundreds of miles away while relying on infernal power, wealth, and patience.

## Connections

- [[Iron Rose]] is her devil-built fortress.
- [[Mammon]] is the archdevil with whom she made her pact.
- [[Godsrain]] created the Warshard opportunity she now exploits.
