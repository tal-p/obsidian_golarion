Adasely Tisandera is an ambitious caravanserai operator on the outskirts of [[Elidir]]. Raised poor in the city's lower levels, she now dreams of controlling every caravanserai under her name and dominating the market.

## Background & Role

Adasely works in one of the trade services that keeps Elidir's wealth moving: lodging, staging, and servicing caravans entering or leaving the capital. Her background among the poor gives her a sharp sense of how much gold passes through the hands of others.

## Ambitions & Influence

She is trying to establish a caravanserai guild and seeks alliances with other merchants who share her appetite for control. She is careful, however, to ensure none of her partners are as ambitious as she is.

## Connections

- [[Elidir]] is the market she hopes to dominate.
- [[Trade Office]] oversees the trade ecosystem her business depends on.
- [[Conerica Landing]] and the city gates feed caravans and merchants toward her business.
