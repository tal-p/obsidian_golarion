Maire Phelan is a jovial storyteller who settled in [[Saringallow]] after years as a traveling bard. Having witnessed the neglect and atrocities of both Isger's government and its Chelaxian master, she uses stories to give people hope.

## Background & Role

Maire is a recent arrival rather than a member of the city's old circles. Her experience elsewhere lets her place local hardship in the wider pattern of [[Cheliax]]'s exploitation and [[Hedvend VI]]'s failures.

## Current Affairs

She regularly performs around Traveler's Square in pleasant weather, earning her keep while serving as an unofficial morning alarm for travelers. Her subtle efforts to probe courier scribe Abdil Nestthew for sympathy toward the [[Hellbreakers League]] revealed his intense dislike of intrigue and conspiracy.

## Ambitions & Influence

Maire wants Saringallow's residents to imagine a future beyond neglect and fear. She advances that purpose through morale, public memory, and hopeful stories rather than direct political leadership.

## Connections

- [[Hellbreakers League]] benefits from her sympathy and quiet intelligence gathering.
- [[Saringallow]] is the audience and adopted home she now tries to strengthen.
