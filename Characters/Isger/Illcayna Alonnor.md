Illcayna Alonnor is a daughter of [[Urgathoa]] who has spent decades spreading undead corruption through rural [[Isger]]. She holds [[Finder's Gulch]] as her base.

## Background & Role

In 4601 AR, Illcayna was still a young priestess of Urgathoa when she fed herself to a group of wights and was resurrected as a daughter of Urgathoa. She slaughtered the villagers of Finder's Gulch, transformed them into undead thralls, and made the haunted quarry town the base for her armies. From there, she has raided Isgeri villages, [[Cheliax]], and [[Molthune]].

## Current Affairs

Her schemes have largely gone unhindered because Isger's government neglects the countryside. She exploited the mass casualties of the [[Goblinblood Wars]] to raise more undead, and after the [[Godsrain]] she collected warshards for her own purposes. She works in the shadows to bring about Urgathoa's plans and hopes eventually to turn Isger's whole populace to her goddess, likely by force.

## Ambitions & Influence

Illcayna's influence is strongest where rural suffering, isolation, and weak government response leave settlements exposed. [[Gillamoor]] shows the scale of catastrophe she can engineer.

## Connections

- [[Finder's Gulch]] is her stronghold.
- [[Gillamoor]] was destroyed by her undead curse.
- [[Urgathoa]] is the divine power behind her work.
