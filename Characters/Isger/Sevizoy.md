Sevizoy is a former assassin and the leader of [[Nocticula]]'s reformed temple in [[Elidir]]. His faith in the Redeemer Queen is intense but still new, and he believes everyone deserves the chance to change as she did.

## Background & Role

Sevizoy has little formal clerical training beyond deep conviction. He leads a small congregation from New Dream Designs, a tattoo shop that secretly shelters the survivors who accepted Nocticula's ascension and rejected her old demonic identity.

## Current Affairs

He is fighting a hidden holy war against traditionalist Nocticulans who hunt his clergy and followers. Sevizoy calls them heretics, yet still hopes they may someday find redemption.

His shrine is developing magical tattoos to sell, hoping to raise enough money to hire mercenaries against [[Mistress Erity]] and the assassins of [[Sweet Embrace]].

## Connections

- [[Sweet Embrace]] is the rival temple and assassin stronghold he opposes.
- [[Mistress Erity]] leads the old Nocticulan faction.
- [[Nocticula]]'s transformation is the source of the schism.
