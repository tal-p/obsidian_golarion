The Queen of Conerica is the mysterious halfling pirate captain currently preying on merchant ships along the [[Conerica River]]. She is a scourge of trade vessels near [[Elidir]], but her identity remains unknown despite a large bounty.

## Background & Role

Her pirates raid only trade ships and have become frighteningly efficient. They complete attacks within an hour and escape with cargo before authorities can catch them.

## Current Affairs

The Queen's raids embarrass [[Hedvend VI]], the [[Trade Office]], and the [[Isgeri Army]], all of whom depend on safe river commerce to keep Cheliax satisfied.

## Connections

- [[Conerica Landing]] is the trade hub most directly threatened by her raids.
- [[Trade Office]] loses revenue and credibility when ships are hit.
- [[Isgeri Army]] patrols the river routes she exploits.
