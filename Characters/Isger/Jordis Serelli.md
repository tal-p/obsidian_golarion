Jordis Serelli is the determined captain of [[Saringallow]]'s city watch. They are no Chelaxian sympathizer, but they distrust insurgent movements and believe even noble rebellions are likely to do more harm than good.

## Background & Role

Jordis grew up in an orphanage and runs the watch with the same strict discipline. From the barracks inside the Noble Folly gate, they command two dozen guards and oversee crimes, pickpockets, graveyard patrols, public disturbances, and the persistent efforts of local children to climb the Noble Folly path.

## Current Affairs

The watch expanded after the demonic corruption crisis at [[Sarini Manor]]. Jordis regularly investigates disturbances near the concealed [[Hellbreakers League]] headquarters, whose neighbors carefully avoid revealing anything.

## Ambitions & Influence

Jordis values order and public safety over ideology. Their opposition to conspiracies places them at odds with Saringallow's rebels without making them loyal to [[Cheliax]].

## Connections

- [[Sandra Trinelli]] presides over the city whose watch Jordis commands.
- [[Hellbreakers League]] operates covertly despite Jordis's scrutiny.
- [[Sarini Manor]] was the center of the crisis that prompted the city's security expansion.
