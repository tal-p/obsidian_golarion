Elleri "Gram" Smalltoes is a mysterious elderly halfling candler in [[Saringallow]] and one of the oldest recruits of the [[Hellbreakers League]]. Locals do not know where she came from and joke that she may be as old as the city itself.

## Background & Role

Gram escaped enslavement before the Emancipation Edict and carries a deep grudge against [[Cheliax]] for its crimes against her and other halflings. She generally keeps to herself except when nagging customers.

## Current Affairs

When the Hellbreakers discovered that an abandoned warehouse they purchased was too conspicuous for a headquarters, Gram proposed retaining it as a decoy. She turned it into the Beacon Candles Workshop, whose sales fund the rebellion.

## Ambitions & Influence

Gram developed a covert communication system in which candle colors, carved patterns, and shapes carry secret messages. Her apparently ordinary craft now supports both the League's finances and its local intelligence network.

## Connections

- [[Hellbreakers League]] relies on her workshop, code, and long memory.
- [[Saringallow]] provides the community in which her inconspicuous trade masks rebel work.
