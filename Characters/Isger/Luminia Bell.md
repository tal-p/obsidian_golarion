Luminia Bell is a cheerful fine dining chef in [[Elidir]] and the secret leader of [[Urgathoa]]'s followers in [[Isger]]. Few know the Pallid Princess has an organized presence in the capital, let alone that Luminia guides it.

## Background & Role

Luminia's public work gives her access to rare delicacies, unusual ingredients, and wealthy patrons. She is popular among Elidir's elites for elaborate meals and unique food decorations.

## Ambitions & Influence

She brings luxuries back to Urgathoa's faithful for feasts and debauchery in the name of gluttony. Her circle encourages elite excess and whispers temptation to the poor, promising that they deserve more and better.

Urgathoa's agents also work against rival faiths by ensuring ambitious clerics find opportunities elsewhere, leaving Elidir served by less capable religious leaders.

## Connections

- [[Kingsthrone Cathedral]] and Coffer Hall are public religious powers her faith works around.
- [[Dwingini Berylbelt]] leads an Abadaran temple weakened by Urgathoan maneuvering.
- [[Xerlidia Park]] preserves an ancient Kellid altar tied to Urgathoa as a harvest goddess.
