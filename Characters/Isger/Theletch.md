Theletch is the owner of Spellbinder's Stage, a small [[Elidir]] theater celebrated by the city's elite for minimalist performances. His work is more subversive than his patrons seem willing to notice.

## Background & Role

Spellbinder's Stage often presents spoken pieces or stage readings because it lacks the resources for grander productions. Elidir's elites mistake that poverty for avant-garde restraint and praise it as a uniquely Isgeri dramatic style.

## Ambitions & Influence

Theletch has responded by making his shows increasingly searing, trusting that his audience will continue overlooking criticism set directly before them. Rumor says the theater intends to perform the infamous play _Abrogail I_ when the timing is right.

## Connections

- [[Elidir]]'s elite society gives him both audience and target.
- [[Cheliax]] and [[House Thrune]] are the powers most likely to object if his work becomes too obvious.
