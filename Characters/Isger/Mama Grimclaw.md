Mama Grimclaw is a devious iron hag who preys on children left vulnerable by war, instability, and rural neglect in [[Isger]]. She keeps her kidnapped victims in [[Good Home]], a hidden town shielded from scrying magic and surrounded by illusions that cause travelers to pass by without noticing it.

## Background & Role

For decades after the [[Goblinblood Wars]], Isger's countryside was full of orphaned children, giving Mama Grimclaw easy victims. She presents Good Home as a place where her stolen children learn self-sufficiency, but that training is part of their prison: the children are taught enough that they never need to leave.

## Current Affairs

Improved village sheriffs and security have made abductions harder, so Mama Grimclaw now orders her current children to create distractions while she takes another victim. Villages warn children about her, but she does not care whether a child is good or bad, only whether the child is easy to kidnap.

The threat of wider war has given her new opportunities to prey on frightened and vulnerable people.

## Ambitions & Influence

Mama Grimclaw's power depends on isolation, fear, and the gaps left by Isger's weak rural protection. She keeps Good Home functioning as a hidden household of captives and sometimes murders travelers who come too close so she can give their belongings to the children.

## Connections

- [[Good Home]] is the abandoned town she renamed and controls.
- [[Goblinblood Wars]] created the orphan crisis that helped her gather victims.
- [[Isger]]'s current instability gives her more chances to hunt.
