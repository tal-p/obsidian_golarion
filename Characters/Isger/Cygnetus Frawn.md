Cygnetus Frawn is Elidir's foremost trendsetter in fashion and society. Their public image is flighty and stylish, but they are a dedicated supporter of [[Cheliax]] who uses elite social life to keep Isger aligned with its patron.

## Background & Role

Cygnetus leads the tastes of [[Elidir]]'s elite through parties, fashion choices, charity balls, masquerades, and public donations to the church of [[Asmodeus]].

## Ambitions & Influence

Their events and trends subtly maneuver nobles and merchants toward pro-Chelaxian positions. What appears to be social frivolity serves a political goal: keeping [[Isger]] in check.

## Connections

- [[Chelaxian Quarter]] reflects the world Cygnetus supports and flatters.
