Dwingini Berylbelt is the archbanker and high priest of [[Abadar]] in [[Elidir]]. She tends the city's Abadaran faithful from Coffer Hall, though she keeps her sharper political judgments private.

## Background & Role

Dwingini serves one of Elidir's most important faiths in a city defined by trade, debt, contracts, and wealth. Coffer Hall is surprisingly restrained given the capital's commerce, partly because [[Urgathoa]]'s agents have helped draw Abadar's most capable clerics elsewhere.

## Ambitions & Influence

She believes Isger's focus on Elidir stifles markets and prosperity in the countryside. She stays quiet because speaking openly would not serve her or her temple well.

## Connections

- Coffer Hall is her temple.
- [[Trade Office]] and [[Conerica Landing]] shape the markets her faith understands best.
- [[Luminia Bell]]'s secret faith benefits from weakening capable Abadaran leadership.
