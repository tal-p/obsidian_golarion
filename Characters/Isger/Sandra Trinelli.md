Sandra Trinelli is the prudent elected mayor of [[Saringallow]], a reformer trying to modernize the growing settlement and prepare it for full cityhood. With less than a year remaining in her term, her program faces resistance from traditionalists and the politically organized owners of the [[Gallow Farms]].

## Background & Role

Trinelli purchased a mansion at Crossroad Plaza for a larger City Hall, turning the old hall over to the watch. She expanded the city's registry, records staff, and rural cartography work and began furnishing a courthouse, although no judge has been appointed.

## Current Affairs

The additional administration proved essential when [[Hedvend VI]] imposed a wartime tax and many residents needed deferrals. Trinelli also wants the city divided into districts with named streets and numbered buildings.

The Gallow Farms blame slums along the north wall and increasing crop theft on her administration and support Gerudina Mezeret as a challenger.

## Ambitions & Influence

Trinelli believes modernization and reform can turn Saringallow into a major city. Her outlook clashes with older residents who prize custom, rustic identity, and superstition.

## Connections

- [[Gallow Farms]] are the center of organized opposition to her reelection.
- [[Jordis Serelli]] commands the expanded city watch.
- Lupia Marietti, Trinelli's former secretary and current City Historian, has uncovered potentially explosive pre-Isgeri records.
