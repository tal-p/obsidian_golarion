Bulric Soddentough runs Myrogy's Menagerie in [[Elidir]], a once-popular showcase of exotic animals and strange creatures that has fallen into disrepair.

## Background & Role

Ordinary citizens still enjoy occasional tours, but the city's elites have grown bored of familiar animals and demand constant novelty before they will offer patronage.

## Current Affairs

Bulric has begun paying adventurers to bring him new creatures regardless of danger. He hopes fresh attractions will restore the menagerie's reputation and lure back wealthy clients.

## Connections

- [[Elidir]]'s jaded elite drive his desperate search for novelty.
- Myrogy's Menagerie sits among the capital's many entertainments and curiosities.
