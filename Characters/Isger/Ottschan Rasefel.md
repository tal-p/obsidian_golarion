Ottschan Rasefel runs the Elidir Academy for Proper Goblins, a former orphanage repurposed into a school for goblins. Their project is popular among the city's elite but harshly received by many of the goblins sent there.

## Background & Role

After the children of the old orphanage grew up and left, Ottschan turned to "educating" goblins as their next civic project. Their severe manner, short temper, and harsh methods drive away many young goblins.

## Connections

- [[Elidir]]'s elites support the academy as goblins become a more visible labor force in the city.
- The academy reflects lingering tension between Goblinblood Wars survivors and newer goblin arrivals.
