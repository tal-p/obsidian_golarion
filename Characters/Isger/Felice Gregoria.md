Felice Gregoria owns the Succubus's Sash, a high-end [[Elidir]] boutique specializing in devil-themed fashion and delayed imports of Egorian trends. They are not truly a Chelaxian loyalist, but they know loyalists spend well.

## Background & Role

Felice caters to elites who want to appear fashionable, infernal, and connected to [[Cheliax]]. The boutique offers costly garments with special magical flourishes, including illusory scents and glimmering images of dancing nobles.

## Ambitions & Influence

Felice's success depends on reading Elidir's elite insecurities and selling them the image they want. The shop's name ignores the difference between succubi and devils, which matters less than the gold brought in by customers chasing infernal style.

## Connections

- [[Chelaxian Quarter]] and Elidir's elite provide their best clientele.
- [[Cygnetus Frawn]] shapes the social world Felice sells to.
