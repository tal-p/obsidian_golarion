Sister Marcella is the most influential member of the [[Sisterhood of the Golden Erinys]] and grand overseer of its charities in [[Isger]].

## Background & Role

Marcella has served the [[Church of Asmodeus]] since childhood. She is an unassuming elderly woman whose authority comes from the sisterhood's strong reputation for rural charity.

## Current Affairs

Through the sisterhood's orphanages and aid work, Marcella has major influence across the countryside. She loves children and is often found reading infernal passages from religious tomes to children too young to understand them.

## Ambitions & Influence

Marcella's influence is soft but extensive: the sisterhood's relief work, orphanages, and training institutions make Asmodean doctrine feel benevolent to many Isgeri who suffered after the [[Goblinblood Wars]].

## Connections

- [[Sisterhood of the Golden Erinys]] is the order she oversees.
- [[Twin Horns]] trains many girls raised in the sisterhood's orphanages.
- [[Vordia]] benefited from the sisterhood's postwar rebuilding.
