Barasthangas is an undead fleshwarp mage who creates new forms of undead with power drawn from the [[Void]]. She regards [[Urgathoa]] as both patron and co-parent of her profane creations.

## Background & Role

Originally an elven mage, Barasthangas became obsessed with destruction and anti-life. A journey into the Void transformed both her body and magic, stretching translucent flesh over visible bones and tissue and leaving her powers entwined with entropic energy.

## Current Affairs

Back in the [[Universe]], Barasthangas experiments on living captives to create unfamiliar undead infused with the Void. She draws life force from enemies to fuel her work and acquire new subjects.

## Ambitions & Influence

Barasthangas treats each creation as a child shared with Urgathoa. Her work combines personal scholarship with devotion, expanding the possible forms that undeath can take.

## Connections

- [[Urgathoa]] is her patron and the divine partner she imagines in her experiments.
- The [[Void]] transformed her and remains the source of her entropic power.
