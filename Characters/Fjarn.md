Fjarn is a hulking ghoul commander and former Ulfen Linnorm King who wages endless war against [[Urgathoa]]'s enemies. His corpse-gray skin and fixed smile have replaced the regal bearing of his mortal reign.

## Background & Role

As a Linnorm King, Fjarn was forced to murder and consume his own honor guard. He died by suicide afterward but rose from the grave with a hunger for the same forbidden flesh.

## Current Affairs

Fjarn leads Urgathoa's wars and devours those who fall before him. Warriors of rival faiths recount the horror of seeing a former king stoop over battlefield carrion.

## Ambitions & Influence

Each campaign gives Fjarn new enemies to destroy and new corpses to consume. He has exchanged mortal kingship for an unending martial existence in the Pallid Princess's service.

## Connections

- [[Urgathoa]] directs the wars that now define him.
- The [[Linnorm Kingdoms]] remember the royal identity he lost to death and hunger.
